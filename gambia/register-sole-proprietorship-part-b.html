1. Service Overview

The Register a Sole Proprietorship service on the Gambia Easy Business portal processes four government registrations simultaneously from a single citizen application. After the citizen submits and pays, each agency's review queue receives the application and processes it in parallel.

Issuing authority: Registrar General’s Department (RGD), Ministry of Justice

The core registration that legally establishes the sole proprietorship. The officer verifies the applicant’s identity and checks that the proposed business name is available in the national business register. Upon validation, an automated bot assigns a Business Registration Number (BRN) and generates the Certificate of Business Registration.

Roles involved: BR Review → BR Approval → BR Registration (bot) → BR Certificate (bot)

Issuing authority: Gambia Revenue Authority (GRA)

Every business operating in The Gambia requires a Tax Identification Number for tax compliance. If the applicant already has a personal TIN registered with GRA, it is automatically linked to the new business. If not, a new TIN is generated. The GRA workflow includes three human steps: Review, Approval, and Signature. Upon completion, the TIN Registration bot updates the GRA database and the TIN Card bot generates the downloadable TIN Card document.

Roles involved: TIN Review → TIN Approval → TIN Signature → TIN Registration (bot) → TIN Card (bot)

Issuing authority: Social Security and Housing Finance Corporation (SSHFC)

Registers the business owner as an employer for Social Security purposes. This allows the business to enroll employees in the national Social Security scheme. Even if the applicant declares no current employees, the registration is processed and can be updated later when staff are hired.

Roles involved: SSHFC Review → SSHFC Approval → SSHFC Registration (bot) → SSHFC Certificate (bot)

Issuing authority: Kanifing Municipal Council (KMC)

Required only if the business address falls within the KMC jurisdiction (Serrekunda, Bakau, Fajara, Kanifing, and surrounding areas). The system automatically determines applicability based on the applicant’s declared business address. The KMC workflow includes a unique KMC Payment step where the officer records the fee payment, followed by the RippleNami payment validator bot before registration.

Roles involved: KMC Review → KMC Approval → KMC Payment → RippleNami TL Payment Validator (bot) → KMC Registration (bot) → KMC Certificate (bot)

Full Timeline

Applicant Submits & Pays (BR Payment)

The citizen completes the sole proprietorship registration form online, pays the applicable fees, and submits. The application simultaneously enters all four agency queues.

Triggered by citizen action

BR Review → BR Approval (RGD)

The Registrar General’s Department reviews the application for completeness, verifies the applicant’s identity against the national ID database, and checks the business name for conflicts. A senior officer gives final sign-off.

Human officers — typically 2–5 working days

BR Registration → BR Certificate (bots)

After BR Approval, the system automatically creates the business record in the national registry, assigns a Business Registration Number (BRN), and generates the Certificate of Business Registration for download.

Automated — minutes after BR Approval

TIN Review → TIN Approval → TIN Signature (GRA)

The Gambia Revenue Authority processes the TIN registration in parallel. Three GRA officers handle review, approval, and final signature. If the applicant already has a registered TIN, the system links it automatically and the process may be shorter.

Human officers — typically 1–3 working days

TIN Registration → TIN Card (bots)

After TIN Signature, bots update the GRA registry with the new TIN and generate the TIN Card document for the citizen.

Automated — minutes after TIN Signature

SSHFC Review → SSHFC Approval

The Social Security and Housing Finance Corporation reviews and approves the employer registration. Runs in parallel with BR and TIN workflows.

Human officers — typically 2–3 working days

SSHFC Registration → SSHFC Certificate (bots)

Bots update the SSHFC employer registry and generate the employer registration certificate.

Automated — minutes after SSHFC Approval

KMC Review → KMC Approval → KMC Payment (KMC) — if applicable

If the business address is within KMC jurisdiction, the Kanifing Municipal Council reviews and approves the trading license application. The KMC Payment step requires the officer to record the trading license fee payment before proceeding.

Human officers — typically 3–7 working days

RippleNami Validator → KMC Registration → KMC Certificate (bots)

After KMC Payment, the RippleNami bot validates the payment. On success, bots update the KMC license registry and generate the Trading License Certificate.

Automated — minutes after KMC Payment confirmation

3. Type of Roles

There are two types of roles in an eRegistrations service:

Assigned to a government officer who manually reviews or approves applications. The officer sees the full application form, the applicant’s details, and the payment confirmation. They can add comments and set a status to advance the file or return it.

Role Statuses

StatusMeaning
FILE PENDINGApplication is queued or in progress at this desk. No action required unless it stalls beyond the expected processing time.
FILE VALIDATEDDesk approved the application. It moves automatically to the next step in the workflow.
SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONSApplication returned to the applicant with officer comments explaining what needs to be corrected. The applicant must fix the issue and resubmit.
FILE REJECTApplication permanently rejected. No further processing occurs. The applicant is notified and must submit a new application.

Executed automatically by the system — no officer action required. Bots run in sequence after the previous step validates. They update the relevant registry database and generate the certificate PDF for the citizen.

Bot Statuses

StatusMeaningAction Required
FILE VALIDATEDBot ran successfullyNone — proceeds to next step automatically
FILE PENDINGBot is queued or currently runningWait a few minutes and refresh
Bot failedBot encountered an errorCheck the error log. The corresponding Problem role becomes available for an officer to investigate. See Section 5.

4. Description of Roles

Use the tabs below to navigate each agency’s roles.

  1. 1

    BR Review

    The RGD reviewing officer opens the application and verifies that all required information is present and correct. Check the following:

    • Applicant’s full name matches their identity document (National ID, Passport, or Driver’s License)
    • Date of birth and nationality are provided and consistent with identity document
    • Business name is provided (own name, reserved name, or proposed name) and the name availability check has been performed
    • Business address and description of activities are complete and plausible
    • Applicant does not already have a registered sole proprietorship (the system flags this, but verify if unclear)
    • Payment confirmed in the system

    If the proposed business name is similar to an existing name, consult the registry. Minor differences (e.g. spelling) may still conflict — use your judgement or consult the Legal Unit.

  2. 2

    BR Approval

    The approving officer (typically a senior RGD officer or supervisor) performs a final check on the reviewed application and confirms the Review Desk has correctly verified all points. If satisfied, set the status to FILE VALIDATED. This immediately triggers the BR Registration bot.

    If there are concerns, use FILE REJECT with a written justification, or send back to the Review Desk for re-examination.

  3. 3

    BR Registration (Bot)

    Runs automatically after BR Approval. Creates a new business record in the national business registry with:

    • Owner’s name, nationality, ID number, and date of birth
    • Business name and registration number (BRN)
    • Business address and description of activities
    • Registration date

    If this bot fails, the Problem BR Registration role becomes active. Investigate the error log before retrying.

  4. 4

    BR Certificate (Bot)

    Runs after BR Registration succeeds. Generates the Certificate of Business Registration PDF showing the BRN, business name, owner’s name, registration date, and business activities. Uploads the certificate to the citizen’s eRegistrations dashboard and sends an email notification.

  1. 1

    TIN Review

    The GRA reviewing officer checks the TIN registration request. Verify:

    • If applicant already has a TIN in the GRA database, the system should have pre-filled it — confirm the existing TIN is correct
    • If no existing TIN, confirm applicant identity details are sufficient for TIN generation
    • Business activity and address are consistent with the BR registration details
  2. 2

    TIN Approval

    The GRA approval officer confirms that the TIN review is complete and all details are accurate. Sets status to FILE VALIDATED to proceed to the Signature step.

  3. 3

    TIN Signature

    A designated GRA officer provides the final authorisation signature on the TIN registration. This is the last human step before the bots run. Upon setting FILE VALIDATED, the TIN Registration bot triggers automatically.

  4. 4

    TIN Registration (Bot)

    Updates the GRA tax registry. If the applicant had no existing TIN, a new TIN number is generated and stored. If an existing TIN was linked, it is associated with the new business record. If this bot fails, the Problem TIN Registration role activates.

  5. 5

    TIN Card (Bot)

    Generates the TIN Card document showing the applicant’s name, TIN number, and registration date. Uploads it to the citizen’s eRegistrations dashboard. If this bot fails, the Problem TIN Certificate role activates.

  1. 1

    SSHFC Review

    The SSHFC reviewing officer verifies the employer registration details. Check:

    • Business owner details match the BR registration data
    • Number of employees declared is plausible given the business type and size
    • Business address and activity description are complete
  2. 2

    SSHFC Approval

    The SSHFC approval officer gives final sign-off on the employer registration. Sets status to FILE VALIDATED to trigger the SSHFC Registration bot.

  3. 3

    SSHFC Registration (Bot)

    Creates the employer record in the SSHFC database, enabling Social Security contributions for the business’s employees. Assigns an SSHFC Employer Registration Number. If this bot fails, the Problem SSHFC Registration role activates.

  4. 4

    SSHFC Certificate (Bot)

    Generates the employer registration certificate and uploads it to the citizen’s dashboard. If this bot fails, the Problem SSHFC Certificate role activates.

Conditional workflow

These roles only appear in the queue if the applicant’s business address is within KMC jurisdiction. If you do not see KMC roles for a specific file, the business address is outside the KMC area — this is expected behaviour.

  1. 1

    KMC Review

    The KMC reviewing officer checks the trading license application. Verify:

    • Business address is within the KMC area and the correct fee category has been applied
    • Business activity category is correct (accommodation, retail, communications, automobile, etc.)
    • Fee calculation parameters (e.g. number of rooms, machines, vehicles, booths) are plausible and match the declared business description
    • Stock value (if applicable) has been correctly declared and the appropriate tariff tier applied
  2. 2

    KMC Approval

    The KMC approval officer confirms the reviewed application is correct and sets it to FILE VALIDATED to proceed to the payment step.

  3. 3

    KMC Payment

    The KMC payment officer records the receipt of the trading license fee payment in the system. This is a manual step — the officer enters the payment reference, amount, and date. Once the payment details are confirmed and the status set to FILE VALIDATED, the RippleNami payment validator bot runs automatically.

    Do not validate without confirmed payment

    Only set FILE VALIDATED on the KMC Payment step once you have confirmed that the KMC fee has actually been received. Premature validation will trigger the registration bot and issue a license before payment is collected.

  4. 4

    RippleNami TL Payment Validator (Bot)

    Automatically validates the KMC trading license (TL) payment recorded in the previous step against the RippleNami payment system. If the payment is confirmed, the bot sets FILE VALIDATED and proceeds to KMC Registration. If validation fails, check the payment reference and contact the KMC finance unit.

  5. 5

    KMC Registration (Bot)

    Creates the trading license record in the KMC registry database with the business name, address, activity category, fee paid, issue date, and expiry date (one year from issue). Assigns a KMC License Number. If this bot fails, the Problem KMC Registration role activates.

  6. 6

    KMC Certificate (Bot)

    Generates the KMC Trading License Certificate showing the business name, KMC License Number, activity category, address, issue date, and expiry date. Uploads to the citizen’s dashboard. If this bot fails, the Problem KMC Certificate role activates.

5. Assistance

Common Issues

IssueLikely CauseRecommended Action
Applicant name does not match identity document Typo in the application form, or applicant used a name different from their registered ID Send back to corrections (BR Review) asking applicant to correct their name to match their official ID document
Proposed business name conflicts with existing registration A very similar or identical name already exists in the business register Send back to corrections with an explanation. The applicant must propose a different, unique name.
Applicant already has a sole proprietorship registered The system should have blocked this, but may have a data mismatch Reject the application with explanation. Direct the applicant to close their existing sole proprietorship first, or to register a company instead.
BR Registration bot failed — “ID not in registry” The applicant’s National ID number is not in the national identity database Activate the Problem BR Registration role. Contact the database administrator to verify. Do not retry until confirmed.
KMC fee calculation appears incorrect Applicant declared the wrong business category or number of units (rooms, machines, etc.) Send back to corrections from KMC Review, asking the applicant to correct the business category or unit count
RippleNami payment validator failed Payment reference entered incorrectly, or payment not yet processed by the bank Verify the payment reference with the KMC finance unit. If correct, wait 1–2 hours and retry. If incorrect, send back to KMC Payment for correction.
TIN bot failed — “Duplicate TIN” The system attempted to create a new TIN but one already exists for this identity document Activate Problem TIN Registration. Contact GRA IT to link the existing TIN to the new business record manually.
Payment confirmed but application still shows “pending payment” Payment gateway delay Wait up to 1 hour. If still pending, verify payment with the finance team and confirm manually if appropriate.
A certificate bot failed — PDF not generated Template error, missing data from the preceding registration bot, or document service unavailable Check that the registration bot completed successfully first. Then retry the certificate bot. If it persists, escalate to IT Support.
