Extend a Name Reservation

Keep your reserved business name secured beyond its initial expiry date. This guide walks you through the online extension process — from confirming your reservation details to receiving your updated certificate.

Introduction

When you reserve a business name in The Gambia, the reservation is valid for a limited period. If you have not yet completed your full business registration before the reservation expires, you can apply to extend the name reservation to give yourself more time.

The extension process is entirely online via the Gambia Easy Business portal. Because your reservation details are already on file, the form is mostly pre-filled — you simply confirm the information, pay the extension fee, and submit. The system will issue an updated reservation certificate once your application is processed.

ⓘ
Who can use this service?

Only the registered owner of the name reservation can extend it. You must be logged in to the Easy Business portal with the account that holds the reservation. If you are not the owner, you will not be able to proceed.

Before You Begin

The extension process is straightforward. Before you start, make sure you have the following ready:

  • ✓
    Your Easy Business account — you must be logged in. The system verifies that you are the owner of the reservation before allowing any action.
  • ✓
    Your existing name reservation — the reservation must be active (not yet expired or cancelled). You can check your reservation status by logging in and viewing your applications dashboard.
  • ✓
    Proof of payment — a scanned copy or photograph (PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, or BMP) of your payment receipt for the extension fee. You will need to upload this during the payment step.
⚠
Apply before your reservation expires

Do not wait until after the expiry date. Once a reservation expires, you may no longer be able to extend it and would need to submit a new reservation application — and the name may have been taken by someone else in the meantime. Apply for an extension as soon as you know you will need more time.

▶
Tip: Log in before you start

Go to services.easybusiness.gov.gm, log in to your account, and navigate to the "Extend a Name Reservation" service. If you are not logged in, the system will redirect you to the login page.

How to pay

The extension fee must be paid to the Business Registry before uploading your receipt. Accepted payment methods include:

  • Bank transfer or counter payment — pay at your bank or at the Business Registry counter, then scan or photograph the stamped receipt.
  • Digital payment — if an online payment option is active on the portal, you may be able to pay directly through the system.

For current payment instructions and bank details, visit easybusiness.gov.gm or contact the Business Registry.

Uploading your payment receipt

Once you have paid, upload your receipt by clicking the upload area or dragging your file onto it. Accepted formats are: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP. Make sure the receipt is clearly legible — blurry or cropped images may delay processing.

⚠
The receipt upload is mandatory

You cannot proceed to the final submission step without uploading a valid proof of payment. Do not upload unrelated documents in this field — it will cause your application to be rejected or sent back for corrections.

After uploading your receipt, click Go to send page to move to the final submission step.

The solemn declaration

By ticking this box you are making a formal legal declaration that the information you have provided is true and correct to the best of your knowledge. This is a statutory requirement for all applications submitted to the Business Registry.

⚠
This is a legally binding statement

Providing false or misleading information in a business registration application is an offence under Gambian law. Only tick this box if you are confident all the information in your application is accurate.

Once you have ticked the declaration box, click Submit application. Your application will be sent to the Business Registry for processing. You will receive an email confirmation at the address associated with your account.

✓
Application submitted successfully

After submission you will see a confirmation screen with your application reference number. Keep this number — it lets you track your application status and contact the Registry if needed.

After Submission

Once your application is submitted, the Business Registry processes it through the following stages. You can track progress at any time by logging in to the Easy Business portal and checking your applications dashboard.

  • 1
    Payment Verification

    A payment officer confirms receipt and validity of your proof of payment. If you paid online, this step may be handled automatically. If you paid at a counter or by bank transfer, an officer will review and record the payment details. Once confirmed, your file moves to the next stage.

  • ⚙
    Reservation Extension (Automated)

    The system automatically updates your business name reservation record with the new expiry date. This is performed by an automated bot — no manual intervention is required at this stage.

  • ⚙
    Certificate Generation (Automated)

    Once the reservation has been updated, the system automatically generates a new Name Reservation Certificate reflecting the extended expiry date.

  • ✓
    Certificate Available

    You will receive an email notification when your updated certificate is ready. Log in to your Easy Business account, open your application, and download the BNR Certificate from your file.

ⓘ
What if there is a problem?

If the automated processing encounters an issue, your file may be assigned to a Registry officer for manual review. You may receive a request to provide additional information or to correct your submission. Follow the instructions in the email notification you receive.

Where to find the current fee

⚠
Fees are non-refundable

Once paid, the extension fee is non-refundable, even if your application is subsequently rejected. Make sure your reservation is still active and that you are applying for the correct name before making payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

My reservation has already expired. Can I still extend it?

In most cases, you cannot extend a reservation that has already expired. The system will only display active reservations for extension. If your reservation has expired, you will need to submit a new "Reserve a Business Name" application. There is no guarantee the name will still be available, so act promptly before the expiry date.

I am the owner of the reservation but I cannot see it in the extension form. What should I do?

First, make sure you are logged in with the same account that was used for the original reservation. If you are logged in with the correct account and still cannot see the reservation, contact the Business Registry support team at easybusiness.gov.gm/help for assistance.

Can I change the business name when extending the reservation?

No. The extension service only extends the validity period of your existing name reservation. You cannot change the reserved name through this form. If you wish to reserve a different name, you must submit a new "Reserve a Business Name" application.

How many times can I extend a name reservation?

The Business Registry's policy on the maximum number of extensions permitted is subject to change. For the most current policy, visit easybusiness.gov.gm or contact the Registry. In general, it is advisable to complete your full business registration rather than repeatedly extending a reservation.

How long does the extension add to my reservation?

The new expiry date is calculated automatically by the system and is shown on the form before you confirm. The system typically extends the reservation by the same period as the original reservation. The exact new expiry date will be displayed in the "New expiry date" field before you submit.

Will I receive a new certificate?

Yes. Once your extension is processed, the system automatically generates an updated Name Reservation Certificate (BNR Certificate) reflecting the new expiry date. You can download it from your Easy Business account dashboard when it is ready.

I uploaded the wrong payment receipt. What can I do?

If your application has not yet been processed, contact the Business Registry support team as soon as possible via easybusiness.gov.gm/help. If a payment officer has already reviewed and rejected your receipt, you may receive a notification to correct and resubmit your proof of payment.

Who do I contact if I need help?

Visit the Help & Support page at easybusiness.gov.gm/help for guidance, FAQs, and contact information for the Business Registry support team.
