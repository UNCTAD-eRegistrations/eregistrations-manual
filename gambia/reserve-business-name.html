Reserve a Business Name

Secure your preferred business name before registering your company or sole proprietorship. This guide walks you through every step of the online reservation process — from checking name availability to receiving your official reservation certificate.

Introduction

The Reserve a Business Name service allows you to secure a unique business name in the Gambia Business Registry before you complete your formal business registration. Think of it as "holding your spot" — once a name is reserved in your favour, no one else can register or reserve that same name during the reservation period.

This service is provided online through Gambia Easy Business, the Government of The Gambia's digital business registration platform. You can complete the entire process from anywhere with an internet connection — no need to visit an office.

Who should use this service?

You should use this service if you:

  • Have chosen a business name and want to make sure it is available before investing time in a full registration
  • Plan to register a sole proprietorship, company, or other business entity and want to lock in your preferred name first
  • Are applying on behalf of someone else (as an authorised representative) who wants a business name reserved

What you will receive

Upon successful approval of your application, the system will automatically generate an official Business Name Reservation Certificate. This certificate confirms that your chosen name is reserved in the Gambia Business Registry for a set period, giving you time to complete your full business registration before the reservation expires.

ℹ
Important to know

A name reservation does not constitute business registration. You still need to complete the relevant registration (e.g., Register a Sole Proprietorship or Register a Company) before you can legally operate your business under this name.

Before You Begin

Gathering everything you need before you start will make the process much faster. Please have the following ready:

  • ✓
    A valid identity document — either your National ID card, Passport, or Driver's License. You will need to enter the document number exactly as it appears on your document.
  • ✓
    Your full legal name — as it appears on your identity document (first name, any middle names, and last name).
  • ✓
    Your date of birth — you will be asked to select this from a calendar picker.
  • ✓
    Your contact details — a working mobile phone number (with country code) and an active email address. The system will send status updates to your email, so make sure it is correct.
  • ✓
    Your Personal TIN (optional) — if you have a Tax Identification Number issued by the Gambia Revenue Authority, have it handy. It is not mandatory for a name reservation but may be pre-filled if you are a registered taxpayer.
  • ✓
    Your proposed business name(s) — think of one or two alternatives in case your first choice is already taken. The name must be unique and not identical or confusingly similar to an existing registered or reserved name.
  • ✓
    The intended business type — you will need to indicate whether the name is being reserved for a sole proprietorship or another business type (e.g., a company or partnership).
  • ✓
    A scanned copy of your identity document (PDF or JPEG, max 5 MB) — you will be asked to upload this as supporting documentation.
▶
Tip: Create your account first

If you have never used Gambia Easy Business before, register for an account at services.easybusiness.gov.gm before starting your application. This will save your progress and allow you to track your application status online.

Step 3 — Business Name Purpose

Before you enter your proposed name, you need to tell the system what type of business the name will be used for. This is important because different business types are registered under different legal frameworks in The Gambia.

Business Name Purpose

Sole Proprietorship — a business owned and operated by one individual

Why does this matter?

The business type you select here determines how the reserved name will be categorised in the Business Registry and which naming rules apply. For example:

  • A Sole Proprietorship name will typically follow naming conventions specific to individual traders.
  • Other business types (such as limited companies or partnerships) may have additional naming requirements, such as including "Ltd" or "LLP" as part of the name.

If you are not yet sure exactly which business structure you will use, choose the option that best matches your intended plan. You can always discuss this with a business registration officer if you need guidance.

ℹ
One reservation per application

Each application covers the reservation of one business name for one business type. If you need to reserve names for multiple business types, you will need to submit separate applications.

How to enter your proposed name

Type your proposed business name in the text field. Enter it exactly as you want it to appear on your reservation certificate, including any capitalisation, punctuation, or spacing. For example: Jollof Trading Enterprise.

How the availability check works

After typing your proposed name, click the "Check availability" button. The system will instantly search two databases:

  • Reserved names — names that other applicants have already reserved and which have not yet expired
  • Registered businesses — names of businesses that are already officially registered in The Gambia

The check looks for both exact matches and names that are confusingly similar to yours. The result will appear directly below the button:

  • Available (green) — Your proposed name is not in the registry. You can proceed with this name.
  • Not available (red) — Your proposed name already exists in the registry. You must enter a different name and check again before you can submit.
⚠
You must check availability before submitting

The system will not allow you to submit your application until you have clicked "Check availability" and received a result for your proposed name. If you change the name after checking, you must check again.

Tips for choosing a good business name

  • Be unique — Avoid names that are identical to or closely resemble an existing registered or reserved business name. Even a different spelling of a well-known name may be rejected.
  • Be descriptive but distinct — A name like "Gambia Travel Services" is very generic and more likely to conflict with existing registrations. A name like "SunPath Travel Agency" is more distinctive.
  • Avoid restricted terms — Certain words like "Government", "National", "Royal", "Bank", or "Insurance" require special approval. If your name includes such terms, the reviewing officer may query it.
  • Plan alternatives — Think of two or three options before you start. If your first choice is not available, you can change the name in the same application and check again.
▶
What if my name isn't available?

Simply clear the name field, type a different name, and click "Check availability" again. You can do this as many times as you like before submitting. The system will only allow you to submit once you have a confirmed "Available" result.

Scanning tips

If you are using a smartphone to photograph your documents, ensure good lighting and place the document on a flat, dark surface. Use a free scanning app for a cleaner result. The reviewing officer needs to be able to read every detail clearly.

Step 6 — Review & Submit

Before submitting, the system will show you a summary of everything you have entered. This is your last chance to check for mistakes before your application is sent to the Business Registry for processing.

What to check before submitting

  • Your full name matches your identity document exactly
  • Your document number is correct
  • Your email address is spelled correctly
  • The proposed business name is exactly as you intend it to appear on your certificate
  • The availability check for your business name shows "Available"
  • All required documents are uploaded and the files are readable

Declaration

Before you can click the final submit button, you must check the declaration box confirming that:

  • The information you have provided is true and correct to the best of your knowledge
  • You understand that providing false or misleading information may result in legal consequences

Read the declaration carefully and only check the box if you agree.

ℹ
After you click Submit

Your application will be assigned a unique reference number. Note this number — you can use it to track your application status at any time by logging into services.easybusiness.gov.gm.

After Submission

Once you have submitted your application, it enters a multi-step review process handled by the Business Registry team. Here is what happens at each stage:

  • 1
    Application Submitted

    Your application is received and assigned a reference number. You will receive a confirmation email. The application is now pending payment confirmation.

  • 2
    BR Payment — Payment Confirmation

    A Payment Officer confirms receipt of the application fee. If you paid online, this step is handled automatically. If you paid at the counter (cash), the Payment Officer will record your payment details. Once confirmed, the file moves to the Review stage.

  • 3
    BR Review — Application Review

    A Review Officer checks your application for completeness and accuracy. They verify your personal information, check the proposed name against the registry, and review your uploaded documents. The officer may:

    • Validate the file and forward it for approval
    • Send it back to you for corrections if information is missing or incorrect
    • Reject it if it does not meet eligibility requirements
  • 4
    BR Approval — Final Approval

    An Approving Officer makes the final decision on your application. They conduct a final check of the name and all supporting documents. They can approve, reject, or send the application back for further corrections.

  • 5
    BR Reservation (Automated)

    Once approved, the system automatically registers your business name reservation in the official Business Names database. No action is required from you at this stage.

  • 6
    BR Certificate (Automated)

    The system automatically generates your official Business Name Reservation Certificate as a PDF document. You will receive an email notification once your certificate is ready.

  • 7
    Certificate Available for Download

    Your reservation certificate is now available in your account. Log in to services.easybusiness.gov.gm, open your application, and download your certificate. Keep it safely — you may need to present it when completing your full business registration.

If your application is sent back for corrections

If the Review or Approval officer finds an issue with your application, you will receive an email notification. Log in to your account, open your application, make the required corrections, and resubmit. The corrections will be reviewed again by the same officer.

If your application is rejected

If your application is rejected, you will receive an email explaining the reason. Common reasons for rejection include:

  • The proposed name is too similar to an existing registered name
  • The name contains restricted or prohibited terms
  • Submitted documents could not be verified

If you believe the rejection is incorrect, contact the Business Registry at easybusiness.gov.gm/help to discuss your options.

Payment can be made through the following methods:

Frequently Asked Questions

How long will my business name be reserved?

The reservation period is specified on your reservation certificate. During this period, no one else can register or reserve the same name. You must complete your formal business registration before the reservation expires, otherwise the name will become available to others.

What if my proposed name is not available?

Simply clear the name field, enter a different proposed name, and click "Check availability" again. You can try as many names as you like. Only names that show as "Available" can be submitted. Consider having two or three alternative names ready before you start.

Can I reserve more than one business name at the same time?

Each application covers one business name. If you wish to reserve multiple names, you will need to submit separate applications for each name and pay the applicable fee for each.

When will I receive my reservation certificate?

Your certificate is generated automatically as soon as your application is approved and the name reservation is recorded in the registry. This typically happens within 2–5 business days of a complete submission. You will receive an email notification and can download the certificate from your account.

What does the reservation certificate allow me to do?

The certificate confirms that your chosen business name is reserved in your name for a defined period. It does not authorise you to operate a business — it simply holds the name while you complete the formal business registration process. You should proceed with registering your business (as a sole proprietorship, company, or other entity) before the reservation expires.

Can I change my proposed name after I have submitted the application?

Once submitted, you cannot change the proposed name unless an officer sends your application back for corrections. If your application is still being processed and you realise you need a different name, contact the Business Registry as soon as possible at easybusiness.gov.gm/help.

What happens if my application is rejected?

You will receive an email with the reason for rejection. Common reasons include the name being too similar to an existing one, containing restricted terms, or issues with verification documents. If you disagree with the decision, you may contact the Business Registry to discuss your options. You are also free to submit a new application with a different name.

Do I need to be a Gambian citizen to reserve a business name?

No. Both Gambian nationals and foreign nationals can apply to reserve a business name in The Gambia. You will need a valid identity document (passport or National ID) regardless of your nationality. The applicable business registration requirements that follow the reservation may differ for non-citizens.

I am applying on behalf of someone else. What do I need?

Select "Someone else" in Step 1 and provide your own personal information as the representative. You will also need to upload an authorisation letter or power of attorney confirming that the person whose name is being reserved has authorised you to act on their behalf. The name on the reservation certificate will be for the person you are representing.

Who do I contact if I need help?

Visit the Help & Support page at easybusiness.gov.gm/help for guidance, FAQs, and contact information for the Business Registry support team.
