1. Service Overview

The Register a Trading License service on the Gambia Easy Business portal lets citizens apply online for an annual KMC Trading License — the operating licence required for any business physically located within the Kanifing Municipal Council (KMC) jurisdiction. After a citizen submits and pays through the portal, the application enters the KMC back-office queue and is processed by three officer desks and two automated bots.

This service is processed entirely by KMC officers. Three desks are operated by humans (KMC Review, KMC Approval, KMC Payment), two are fully automated (KMC Registration, KMC Certificate), and the Applicant role handles the feedback loop when corrections are requested.

What the citizen receives

A digitally generated, signed KMC Trading License Certificate in PDF format, valid for one year, uploaded to the citizen’s portal account at the end of the workflow.

Registry rows created on completion

When the workflow completes successfully, the following entries are written to the GDB registry databases by the KMC Registration bot chain:

  • TRADING LICENSES (6.6) — one new licence record with the assigned licence N°, issued/renewal dates, activities, and total fee.
  • TRADING LICENSE FEES (4.4) — the invoice payment record, back-linked to the new licence N°.
  • KMC PAYMENTS (1.9) — one row per instalment payment received at the Payment desk.
  • NATURAL PERSONS (6.9) — created or updated for the applicant and (when applicable) the representative.
  • RELATIONS RIGHTS (5.1) — one or two rights entries linking the applicant (and representative, if any) to the new licence.
Same workflow for all owner types

The same six-desk workflow processes applications submitted by a natural person owner, by a representative acting for someone else, and for businesses already registered with the Business Registry or GRA. What differs is which prerequisite documents the Review desk checks, and whether the representative-related bots run at the Registration desk. The desk sequence and statuses are identical.

Applicant Submits & Pays

The citizen completes the application form online (License Owner, Business Details, License Details tabs), uploads any required documents, pays the application fee, and submits. The file enters the KMC back-office queue at sort order 0.

Triggered by citizen

KMC Review (UserRole, sort 0, start)

A KMC Review officer opens the file and verifies the declared activities, cross-checks the business address against KMC jurisdiction, and calculates schedule fees per activity row. They write a comment for the approving officer and forward, or send back / reject.

Human officer — typically 1–3 working days
Branches: SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS → back to Applicant. FILE REJECT → back to Applicant (terminal-style).

KMC Approval (UserRole, sort 1)

A senior officer re-checks the activity grid and fees, decides whether to Issue invoice, captures the invoice N° / issue date / due date, and forwards to KMC Payment.

Human officer — typically 1–2 working days after Review
Branches: SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS → internal loop back to KMC Review (not to the applicant). FILE REJECT → back to Applicant.

KMC Payment (UserRole, sort 2)

The applicant pays the invoice through the portal; for each instalment row the Payment officer confirms receipt. When the “Total remaining” field reaches zero, the officer captures the License N°, issued date, and renewal date, then validates the file.

Human officer — depends on applicant payment speed
Branches: SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS or FILE REJECT → back to Applicant (rare — only for payment-reference issues or fraud).

KMC Registration (BotRole, sort 3)

Six data bots run in sequence to write the licence into the registry databases (NATURAL PERSONS, TRADING LICENSE FEES, TRADING LICENSES, RELATIONS RIGHTS) and back-link payment rows to the new licence N°.

Automated — typically seconds; runs immediately after Payment validates

KMC Certificate (BotRole, sort 4, end)

The KMC Certificate upload document bot generates the signed KMC Trading License Certificate PDF using the licensing certificate template and uploads it to the applicant’s file. The applicant is notified and can download the certificate from their dashboard.

Automated — runs after KMC Registration succeeds

Applicant Feedback Loop (UserRole, sort 299)

When a desk sends a file back, it lands on the Applicant role. The applicant sees the officer comment on a banner, edits the relevant tabs, re-uploads documents if asked, and resubmits — which routes the file back to KMC Review.

Human applicant — only when a correction is requested

3. Type of Roles

This service has two types of roles. The Trading License workflow uses both.

A UserRole is assigned to a logged-in officer (or, for the Applicant role, to the citizen). The user sees a role form with editable fields, the file’s shared data, and a set of action buttons that map to status transitions.

Human-handled desks in this service

  • KMC Review — first officer desk. Verifies the declaration, computes schedule fees, captures inspection notes, and writes a comment for Approval.
  • KMC Approval — senior officer desk. Re-checks fees, decides whether to issue the invoice, and captures invoice metadata.
  • KMC Payment — finance desk. Confirms each invoice instalment and captures the issued/renewal dates and licence N°.
  • Applicant — feedback-loop role. Receives files sent back for corrections, edits and resubmits.

Statuses available at human desks

StatusMeaning & effect
FILE PENDINGDefault state on arrival. Officer is investigating or waiting on data. Don’t leave files in this state for long.
FILE VALIDATEDDesk approved the file. Moves forward to the next desk automatically.
SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONSFile returned with comments. Destination depends on the desk — Approval sends back to Review, others send back to the Applicant.
FILE REJECTApplication refused. Routed back to the Applicant; applicant must submit a new application.

A BotRole is executed automatically by the system — no officer action required. Bots run in sequence after the previous desk validates the file. The service uses 25 bots in total, grouped into four categories below.

Bot categories in this service

  1. D

    Data bots (read)

    Read records from a GDB database into the current file. Used at the human desks to pre-populate fields.

    • NATURAL PERSONS read owners registered ID and email and address — reads applicant identity from GDB NATURAL PERSONS (6.9).
    • v-RIGHTS read business details — reads active businesses linked to the user from VIEW-RIGHTS.
    • KMC FEES read fee and multiplier — Review — reads schedule fee per activity from GDB KMC FEES (1.7) at the Review desk.
    • KMC FEES read fee and multiplier — Approval — re-reads KMC FEES at the Approval desk.
  2. D

    Data bots (create / update)

    Write or update records in a GDB database. Concentrated at the KMC Registration desk — they are the bots that create the actual licence in the registry.

    • KMC PAYMENTS save payment instalments — KMC Payment — writes each instalment row to GDB KMC PAYMENTS (1.9).
    • NATURAL PERSONS create or update — applicant — writes the applicant’s identity into GDB NATURAL PERSONS.
    • NATURAL PERSONS create or update — representative — same, for the representative (when applicable).
    • TRADING LICENSE PAYMENTS create or update — writes the invoice payment record to GDB TRADING LICENSE FEES (4.4).
    • TRADING LICENSES create — creates the licence row in GDB TRADING LICENSES (6.6).
    • RIGHTS create trading licence relations — applicant — rights row linking the applicant to the new licence.
    • RIGHTS create trading license relations — representative — rights row linking the representative (when applicable).
    • TRADING LICENSE PAYMENTS update entries with license nr — back-links payment rows to the new licence N°.
  3. I

    Internal bots (copy / flag)

    Move data between fields within the file, or set flags that control conditional content. They don’t touch external databases.

    • FORM copy — sectors and activities to KMC Review — copies the applicant’s activity grid into the reviewer’s editable grid.
    • KMC REVIEW copy — sectors and activities to KMC Approval — pushes Review’s validated grid into the Approval form.
    • KMC APPROVAL send — corrected sectors and activities to KMC Review — returns corrected rows on send-back.
    • KMC APPROVAL copy — sent back for corrections — sets the “sent back” flag visible on Part A.
    • KMC PAYMENTS copy — amount paid — propagates the running total into the totals panel.
    • KMC PAYMENTS copy — 1st payment date to license issued date — initialises issued date from the first instalment.
    • FORM copy — license details to certificates — mirrors the activity grid into the Part A “license details after invoice paid” section.
    • INTERNAL test — bring values to certificate in part A — diagnostic helper used in test environments.
  4. P

    Document bots (PDF generation)

    Generate a PDF from a print template and either display it (for officer download on demand) or upload it as a final certificate.

    • KMC Report display — generates a printable inspection report on demand at the Review desk.
    • KMC Report 2 display — alternative inspection report template available at Review.
    • KMC Invoice display — generates the printable KMC invoice PDF at the Approval desk.
    • KMC Certificate display — on-demand certificate render (for re-downloads).
    • KMC Certificate upload — generates and uploads the final signed Trading License Certificate PDF at the KMC Certificate desk.

Statuses available at bot desks

StatusMeaningAction required
FILE VALIDATEDBot chain ran successfullyNone — proceeds to next desk automatically.
FILE PENDINGBot is queued or runningWait; typically completes in seconds.
Bot failedOne of the bots returned an errorOpen the audit log, identify which bot failed, follow the troubleshooting steps in § 5.

4. Description of Roles

The six roles below are listed in workflow order. Each step describes what arrives at the desk, what the role does, the available status transitions, and the bots that fire at that desk.

  1. 1

    KMC Review (UserRole · sort 0 · start)

    The first officer-handled desk. A freshly submitted application arrives with the three applicant tabs (License Owner, Business Details, License Details) filled in. The reviewer’s job is to verify the declaration, compute fees, and forward to Approval.

    What you do here:

    • Read the applicant’s three tabs end-to-end.
    • Confirm the business address falls within the KMC jurisdiction.
    • Cross-check declared activities against KMC records / inspection notes.
    • For each row in the Trading Licence grid, look up the schedule fee and record any multiplier (the KMC FEES bot pre-fills these but you can override).
    • Capture inspection details (date, zone) if a site inspection was carried out.
    • Write a comment for the approving officer summarising your findings.

    The detail of what to check depends on the type of owner / business declared:

    • Owner name, ID, email and address match the NATURAL PERSONS GDB record (cross-checked by the read bot).
    • Address of individual is in The Gambia (region/district filled) or, if foreign, the country field is set.
    • Declared activities map onto valid KMC FEES schedule rows; no missing sector/activity combinations.
    • The Business Registry (BR) number is filled and looks up correctly via the v-RIGHTS bot.
    • The applicant has rights on the named business (RELATIONS RIGHTS lookup) — otherwise treat as representative.
    • Business category and the activity grid match what’s permitted under the BR registration.
    • The TIN N° is filled and matches the business name and address on the file.
    • If the business is also registered with the Business Registry, both BR and TIN are filled.
    • Stock value — if the business is in a retail category, the appropriate band determinant is set.

    Status transitions available:

    • FILE VALIDATED → KMC Approval. Use when the declaration is accurate and fees are correctly computed.
    • SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS → Applicant. Use when the declaration is incomplete or inconsistent and the applicant must amend & resubmit.
    • FILE REJECT → Applicant. Use only when the application is not eligible (wrong jurisdiction, fraudulent declaration).
    • FILE PENDING — default state while investigating. Don’t leave files here for long.

    Bots that fire at this desk: FORM copy — sectors and activities to KMC Review (internal copy on arrival); KMC FEES read fee and multiplier — Review (data read); NATURAL PERSONS read owners registered ID and email and address (data read); v-RIGHTS read business details (data read); KMC Report display, KMC Report 2 display (document bots, on demand).

  2. 2

    KMC Approval (UserRole · sort 1)

    A senior officer’s desk. Files validated by Review arrive here. You see the full declaration, the reviewer’s computed fees, their inspection notes, and their comment. Your job is to confirm the fee calculation and decide whether to issue the invoice.

    What you do here:

    • Re-check the activity grid and fees prepared by the reviewer (any field can be overridden — leave a comment if you do).
    • Validate the total cost computation against the KMC fee schedule.
    • Decide whether to Issue invoice = Yes (proceeds) or = No (holds the file).
    • Generate the invoice number and pick the invoice issue / due dates.
    • Print the invoice PDF (KMC Invoice display bot) so it’s available to the applicant on their dashboard.

    Status transitions available:

    • FILE VALIDATED → KMC Payment. Use when invoice is issued and fees are confirmed.
    • SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS → KMC Review (internal loop). Use when Review made a mistake in the grid or fee calculation — this does not go to the applicant.
    • FILE REJECT → Applicant. Use when there are substantive issues that Review couldn’t catch (e.g. ineligibility detected at this level).
    • FILE PENDING — default during your review.

    Bots that fire at this desk: KMC REVIEW copy — sectors and activities to KMC Approval (internal copy on arrival); KMC FEES read fee and multiplier — Approval (data re-read); KMC Invoice display (document bot, on “Print invoice”); KMC APPROVAL send — corrected sectors and activities to KMC Review (internal, on send-back); KMC APPROVAL copy — sent back for corrections (internal flag bot).

  3. 3

    KMC Payment (UserRole · sort 2)

    The finance desk. Approved files with an issued invoice arrive here. The applicant pays the invoice through the portal in one or more instalments. Your job is to confirm each instalment and validate the file when the invoice is fully paid.

    What you do here:

    • For each instalment recorded in the “KMC payments” grid (Payment N°, date, amount paid, remaining), click Confirm payment to mark it received.
    • The “Total remaining” auto-recomputes after each confirmation; when it hits zero the “Fully paid” flag triggers.
    • Capture the License N°, License issued date, License renewal date (one year later), and KMC Registrar name.
    • Only when “Total remaining” is exactly zero, click Validate application — this hands the file off to the automated KMC Registration desk.

    Status transitions available:

    • FILE VALIDATED → KMC Registration (bot). Use only when the invoice is fully paid and all licence-finalisation fields are captured.
    • SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS → Applicant. Rare — e.g. a payment-reference issue the applicant must clarify.
    • FILE REJECT → Applicant. Use only for serious non-payment or fraud cases beyond your remit.
    • FILE PENDING — default while waiting for further instalments.

    Bots that fire at this desk: KMC PAYMENTS save payment instalments — KMC Payment (data create); KMC PAYMENTS copy — amount paid (internal copy); KMC PAYMENTS copy — 1st payment date to license issued date (internal copy); FORM copy — license details to certificates (internal copy, on validate).

    Don’t validate early

    If you click Validate application while “Total remaining” is greater than zero, the KMC Registration bot chain will fail (the licence record requires a non-zero paid total). Wait until the balance is exactly zero.

  4. 4

    KMC Registration (BotRole · sort 3)

    The first automated desk. No human form. A chain of six data bots runs in sequence to write the licence into the registry databases and link it to the applicant’s rights records. Runs immediately after KMC Payment validates a fully-paid file.

    Bots that run, in order:

    1. NATURAL PERSONS create or update — representative — only when the application was submitted by someone other than the owner. Writes to GDB NATURAL PERSONS (6.9).
    2. TRADING LICENSE PAYMENTS create or update — creates the invoice payment record in GDB TRADING LICENSE FEES (4.4).
    3. TRADING LICENSES create — creates the licence row in GDB TRADING LICENSES (6.6) with the assigned licence N°, dates, activities, and total fee.
    4. RIGHTS create trading licence relations — applicant — creates the rights row linking the applicant to the new licence in GDB RELATIONS RIGHTS (5.1).
    5. RIGHTS create trading license relations — representative — same, for the representative when applicable (skipped otherwise).
    6. TRADING LICENSE PAYMENTS update entries with license nr — back-links the payment rows to the newly assigned licence N°.

    Conditional execution: bots #1 and #5 only run when the determinant “Application for someone else” is true. If the applicant submitted for themselves, those two are skipped.

    Status transitions available:

    • FILE VALIDATED → KMC Certificate (bot). All bots completed; licence is in the registry.
    • FILE PENDING — bot chain in progress (seconds).
    • Bot failed — one of the bots returned an error. File paused for support intervention; see § 5.
  5. 5

    KMC Certificate (BotRole · sort 4 · end)

    The final automated desk. A single document bot generates the signed KMC Trading License Certificate PDF and uploads it to the applicant’s file. After this runs, the application is complete — the applicant downloads the certificate from their dashboard.

    Bot that runs:

    • KMC Certificate upload (document · document_generate_and_upload) — renders the “KMC Licensing Certificate — Trading License” template and attaches the resulting PDF to the file.

    The certificate template covers all 13 activity-row variants (Per room, Per truck, Per machine, Per booth, Per show, Per premise, Per boat, Per cold store, Per borehole, Per billboard, Per vehicle, Per CU M³, Per plant) so the PDF renders correctly regardless of the declared activities.

    Status transitions available:

    • FILE VALIDATED — certificate uploaded, application reaches final state, applicant is notified.
    • FILE PENDING — PDF generation in progress (seconds).
    • Bot failed — PDF generator error. Usually a template issue or a missing data field. Escalate to support.
  6. 6

    Applicant feedback loop (UserRole · sort 299)

    The Applicant role isn’t a desk officers staff — it represents the citizen’s side of the feedback loop. When KMC Review, KMC Approval (only for rejects), or KMC Payment sends a file back via SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS or FILE REJECT, the file lands here.

    What happens at this role:

    • The applicant opens the application from their portal dashboard.
    • They see the officer’s comment on a prominent banner (driven by the applicantSentBackForCorrectionsKmcReview determinant and the kmcReviewCommentForApprovingOfficer / kmcApprovalCommentForApprovingOfficer comment fields).
    • They edit the relevant tab(s), re-upload documents if requested.
    • They resubmit — which routes the file back to KMC Review.

    Status transitions available:

    • FILE VALIDATED — resubmission. File moves back to KMC Review.
    • FILE PENDING — default while the applicant prepares the corrections.

    No bots fire at this role — the comment / flag fields are written by the officer-desk bots when the send-back action is taken.

5. Assistance

Common Issues

The table below lists the most frequent issues KMC officers encounter when processing Trading License applications, with the likely cause and the recommended action.

Issue Likely cause Recommended action
Business address not in KMC jurisdiction Applicant mistakenly applied to KMC when their business is in Banjul or Brikama At Review, use FILE REJECT with a clear note pointing the applicant to the correct municipality.
Declared activity count is implausible (e.g. 200 rooms at a residential address) Data-entry error, or possible mis-declaration to lower fee Trigger a site inspection. Use SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS rather than guessing; document the inspection date and zone before re-validating.
Schedule fee mismatch between Review and Approval Reviewer applied the wrong schedule row, or KMC FEES bot read an outdated value At Approval, override the fee directly and document why in the comment. If systematic, escalate to KMC Trading Licenses Unit.
Applicant claims overcharging on the schedule fee Misunderstanding of multiplier rules, or genuine schedule error Hold the file at Payment while clarification is sought from the KMC Trading Licenses Unit head.
“Bot failed” at KMC Registration — duplicate license number License N° collision (rare); the Payment desk regenerated the same N° that’s already in GDB TRADING LICENSES Have the Payment officer reopen the file, regenerate the License N° on the role form, save, then retry the bot from the audit panel.
“Bot failed” at KMC Certificate — PDF not generated Template error or missing required data field on the file Open the audit log to see the template error. If transient, retry. If persistent (e.g. an activity variant not covered), escalate to technical support with the audit reference.
Payment confirmed by applicant but Payment desk shows pending Payment gateway delay between the portal and the KMC ledger Wait up to 1 hour. If still pending, verify with KMC Finance and manually confirm the instalment row if appropriate.
GDB lookup mismatch — BR/TIN exists but doesn’t match the applicant Stale RGD/GRA data, or applicant is using a business they don’t have rights on Do not validate the file. Refer to RGD or GRA liaison, depending on which database the mismatch is in. Send back to corrections if the applicant needs to provide proof.

General principles
  • Always read the previous desk’s comment before validating — many errors are caught by following the trail upstream.
  • Use SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS in preference to FILE REJECT when the issue is fixable. Reject is for substantive ineligibility only.
  • Don’t click Validate at KMC Payment until “Total remaining” is exactly zero. Forwarding early triggers a bot failure at KMC Registration.
  • The activity grid auto-fills via internal copy bots, but any field can be overridden. When you override, leave a comment explaining why.
