User Manual — Applicant

Register a Trading License

Apply online for your KMC Trading License through the Kanifing Municipal Council. Declare your business activities, get your invoice, pay through the portal, and download your signed trading license certificate — all from your Gambia Easy Business account.

1. Service Overview

The Register a Trading License service on the Gambia Easy Business portal lets you apply online for a KMC Trading License — the annual operating licence required for businesses physically located within the Kanifing Municipal Council (KMC) jurisdiction (Serrekunda, Bakau, Fajara, Kanifing, Bakoteh, Tallinding, Latrikunda, and surrounding communities).

You complete one online form describing your business and the activities you carry out (e.g. how many rooms in your guesthouse, how many telecentre booths, how many trucks). The KMC reviews your declaration, calculates the fee, issues an invoice that you pay through the portal, and finally generates your signed Trading License Certificate — which you download from your dashboard.

Who can use this service?

Any individual or representative wishing to register a Trading License for a business physically located within KMC. The business itself must already be registered with the Business Registry (RGD) and have a TIN with the GRA. If your business is not yet registered, use the Register a Sole Proprietorship service first — it bundles the Trading License with all required registrations.

What you receive at the end

A digitally generated KMC Trading License Certificate valid for one year, accessible from your Gambia Easy Business dashboard. You can re-download or print it at any time during its validity.

Annual renewal required

The KMC Trading License is valid for one calendar year. You must renew it annually to continue operating legally. The portal will notify you before your licence is due to expire.

How the System Works

When you log in, the system retrieves your personal details from your profile and (when available) from existing registries. You declare your business activities using a flexible "Trading licence" grid — one row per activity, with only the relevant counter field (rooms, booths, machines, etc.) shown for the activity you pick. The KMC officer reviews your declaration, the system calculates the fee using the KMC schedule, and the invoice is issued. Once you pay the full amount (in one go or by instalments), two automation bots write the licence into the registry and generate your signed PDF certificate.

2. Before You Start

Make sure you have everything ready before beginning your application. Requirements depend on whether your business is already registered with the Business Registry / GRA.

What You Need — Business already registered

  • A registered account on services.easybusiness.gov.gm
  • A valid identity document: National ID, Passport, or Driver’s License
  • Your Business Registration Number from the Registrar General’s Department
  • Your business’s TIN from the Gambia Revenue Authority
  • The business’s physical address in the KMC area and a description of its activities
  • An accurate count of premises / rooms / machines / vehicles / etc. — whatever applies to your activity
  • A bank card or digital payment method to pay the KMC fee after the invoice is issued

Estimated Processing Time

If your business is not yet registered

This service handles the Trading License only. You cannot apply if the business has not been registered with the Business Registry and GRA. Start with one of these one-stop services instead — they include the Trading License as part of a single application:

  • Register a Sole Proprietorship — for individual-owned businesses. Creates the BR, TIN, Employer Registration, and KMC Trading License in one go.
  • Register a Company — for limited liability companies operating in KMC.
Use this service when…

You’re an existing registered business applying for a KMC Trading License on its own — either because you’re a new entrant into the KMC jurisdiction, you’ve added a licensable activity, or you’re registering separately from your business registration.

Have your activity counts ready

The KMC fee is calculated as a per-unit schedule fee multiplied by the count you declare (rooms, booths, machines, vehicles, etc.). Over-declaring costs you more; under-declaring will be caught during KMC Review and the application will be sent back. Save time by gathering accurate figures before you start.

3. Choose Your Application Type

At the start of the application form, you will be asked who the licence is being registered for. Use the tabs below to understand the two options before you begin.

Apply on your own behalf

Choose this option if you are the business owner applying for your own Trading License. Most fields on the “License Owner” tab will be pre-filled from your profile, including your name, ID number, email, and personal TIN (if previously registered with the GRA).

This option is ideal if you are:

  • A sole proprietor managing your own business
  • A director or partner registering the licence directly in your name
  • An individual entrepreneur applying for a licence on your own account
Automatic rights linkage

Once issued, the licence is linked to your profile in the KMC rights register. You will be able to manage it (renew, view, download) directly from your dashboard.

Apply on behalf of a business owner

Choose this option if you are an agent, manager, or representative filing the licence application on behalf of the actual business owner. You enter the owner’s identity and contact details on the “License Owner” tab.

This option is ideal if you are:

  • An accountant or business consultant submitting the application for your client
  • A manager filing on behalf of the owner
  • A family member or business partner authorised to apply on the owner’s behalf
Choose your access carefully

When you choose “Someone else”, an extra question appears: “Will you receive user rights?”. If you tick Yes, you (the representative) will keep ongoing access to manage the licence after issue. If you tick No, only the business owner will see the licence on their dashboard.

Same workflow either way

Both choices follow exactly the same KMC review, approval, payment, and certificate steps. The only differences are: (a) which name/ID fields you fill on the License Owner tab, and (b) the additional “user rights” question for representatives.

5. Fees & Costs

The Trading License fee is set by the KMC Trading Licenses Schedule. You don’t calculate it yourself — the system looks up your sector and activity, applies the multiplier (rooms, booths, machines, etc.), and shows you the total on the invoice issued after KMC Approval. All amounts are in Gambian Dalasi (GMD).

Fee structure

Each activity has a base fee per unit, multiplied by the number of units you declare. Examples:

SectorActivityCounter declaredFee basis
AccommodationPer roomNumber of roomsPer-room schedule fee × rooms
AutomobilePer plantNumber of plantsPer-plant schedule fee × plants
AutomobilePer truckNumber of trucksPer-truck schedule fee × trucks
CarpentryPer machineNumber of machinesPer-machine schedule fee × machines
CommunicationsPer boothNumber of boothsPer-booth schedule fee × booths
EntertainmentPer machine / per showNumber of machines / showsSchedule fee × quantity
Financial InstitutionsPer premiseNumber of premisesPer-premise schedule fee × premises
Printing & StationeryPer machineNumber of machinesPer-machine schedule fee × machines
Sea foodPer boat / per cold storeNumber of boats / cold storesSchedule fee × quantity
TradesmenPer machineNumber of machinesPer-machine schedule fee × machines
UtilitiesPer boreholeNumber of boreholesPer-borehole schedule fee × boreholes
Other businessesPer vehicle / per billboard / per m³Number of vehicles / billboards / m³Schedule fee × quantity

Stock value bands (for retail activities)

For certain retail categories, the fee depends on your declared stock value. The KMC officer captures this at the Review stage. There are three bands used in the schedule:

  • Stock value ≤ 100,000 GMD — lowest band
  • Stock value 100,001–200,000 GMD — mid band
  • Stock value > 200,000 GMD — highest band

Partial payments

The KMC permits payment by instalments. After the invoice is issued you can pay in one go or split the amount into multiple payments. Each instalment is tracked on the portal and the “remaining to be paid” balance updates as you go. Your licence is only finalised once the full invoice amount has been paid.

Multiple activities, one invoice

You can declare multiple activities in one application (e.g. an accommodation business with rooms AND a communications booth on the same premise). The system adds them up into one total invoice. Each row in the “Trading licence” grid is one activity.

Example

Mariama runs a guesthouse in Bakoteh with 6 rooms and a single communications booth. She declares two activity rows: “Accommodation — Per room” with 6, and “Communications — Per booth” with 1. The KMC officer applies the schedule fee to each row, totals them, and issues a single invoice. Mariama pays in two instalments through the portal.

    How the “Trading licence” grid works

    • Sector — broad business category (Accommodation, Automobile, Carpentry, Communications, Entertainment, Financial Institutions, Other businesses, Printing & Stationery, Sea Food, Tradesmen, Utilities).
    • Activity — the specific licensable activity within the sector. The dropdown filters based on the chosen sector. Examples: “Per room”, “Per truck”, “Per machine”, “Per booth”, “Per premise”, “Per show”, “Per boat”, “Per cold store”, “Per borehole”, “Per billboard”, “Per vehicle”, “Per CU M³”, “Per plant”.
    • Counter — the actual quantity. Only the counter that matches your chosen activity is visible.
    • Add another activity — click to add a new row for a different sector/activity.
    Be accurate

    Your declared counts will be verified by the KMC officer during the Review step. If the numbers look implausible or don’t match what KMC sees on the ground, the application will be returned for correction. Over-declaring also costs you more in fees.

  5. 5

    Documents page

    After the three form tabs, you’ll reach the Documents page. The system shows the documents you’ve already attached on the License Owner tab plus any additional documents required by KMC for your specific activities.

    • Documents already on file (ID, TIN card) are shown as pre-attached.
    • If something extra is needed, the page lists it with an upload box.
    • Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG. Maximum size per file: typically 5 MB.

    When everything required is in place, click Next to proceed to the review and submission step.

  6. 6

    Review & submit your application

    The portal shows a final summary of everything you entered: owner details, business details, declared activities, and uploaded documents. Read carefully — this is the version that goes to KMC.

    • Use the Edit links to go back and correct any field.
    • Tick the declaration checkbox confirming the information is true.
    • Click Submit application to send the file to KMC Review.
    No payment at submission

    Unlike other services, you do not pay at the time of submission. KMC must first review your declaration and issue an invoice. You’ll be invited to pay later, from your dashboard, once KMC Approval is done.

  7. 7

    Wait for the KMC invoice & pay

    Your file goes through two officer desks at KMC: KMC Review (a reviewing officer checks the declaration and proposes the fee) and KMC Approval (a senior officer validates the fee and issues the invoice).

    When the invoice is ready, you receive a notification by email and on your dashboard. Open the application and you’ll see the Invoice N°, the Total KMC fees due (in GMD), a “Print invoice” button, and a payment due date. Pay through the portal using your preferred payment method — in one go, or by instalments. The remaining balance updates after each payment.

    Why is my licence not issued yet?

    The licence is only generated once the full invoice amount has been paid. While there’s an outstanding balance, the file stays at the KMC Payment desk. Once the balance reaches zero, the file automatically advances to certificate generation.

  8. 8

    Download your Trading License certificate

    After the final payment, the KMC Registration bot writes your licence into the KMC Trading Licenses database and the KMC Certificate bot generates a signed PDF certificate. You receive a notification, and the certificate appears on your application dashboard with an “Issued” status.

    Open your application to:

    • Download the KMC Trading License Certificate (PDF, signed)
    • See your Licence Number, Issued date, and Renewal date
    • Print or share the certificate as needed
    Keep it accessible

    Display a copy of your trading licence at your business premises — KMC inspectors may ask to see it during routine inspections.

7. After Submission

Once submitted, your application moves through six desks — three reviewed by humans (KMC officers), two handled by automation bots, and one back to you to pay. You can track the status of your file on your dashboard at any time.

Workflow Timeline

KMC Review

A KMC reviewing officer opens your file, checks declared activities against records, looks up the appropriate licensing fees in the schedule, and validates the application. They may also enter inspection details (date of inspection, zone, etc.) and a recommended stock value if your activity is retail-based.

2–3 working days — status: FILE PENDING then FILE VALIDATED

KMC Approval

A senior officer reviews the reviewer’s work, confirms the total cost, sets “Issue invoice” to Yes, and finalises the invoice number. The system generates a printable invoice. The officer may also send the file back to KMC Review for corrections.

1–2 working days — status: FILE VALIDATED → KMC Payment

You: pay the invoice

The portal notifies you that the invoice is ready. You log in, see the amount and due date, and pay through the portal — in one or several instalments. Each payment is tracked on the application page.

As soon as you’re ready — status: FILE PENDING at KMC Payment

KMC Payment confirmation

The Payment officer confirms each instalment as it arrives. When the remaining balance reaches zero, the officer validates the application and it moves on automatically.

Same day after each instalment — status: FILE VALIDATED on full payment

KMC Registration (automated)

An automation bot writes the licence into the KMC Trading Licenses database, creates the relations between you (or the representative) and the licence in the rights register, and links payments to the new licence number.

Automatic, seconds after full payment — bot-driven

KMC Certificate generation (automated)

A document bot generates the signed Trading License Certificate PDF using your data and the KMC template, then uploads it to your dashboard as a downloadable file.

Automatic, seconds after the previous step

Completion — certificate available

Your KMC Trading License certificate is now available from your dashboard. You receive an email notification and the application status shows as Issued / Validated.

Final state — renew next year

8. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a KMC Trading License?

It’s the annual operating licence required by the Kanifing Municipal Council for any business that carries out a commercial activity inside KMC. Without it, KMC inspectors can shut down your operation or fine you for trading without a licence.

How long is the licence valid?

The Trading License is valid for one year from the date of issue. You must renew it each year before the renewal date shown on your certificate.

Can I apply if my business is outside the KMC area?

No. This service is for businesses physically located within KMC. If your business is in Banjul, Brikama, or another council, contact your local municipal authority — KMC has no jurisdiction over you.

Can I apply on behalf of someone else?

Yes. Choose “Someone else” on the License Owner tab and enter the owner’s details. You’ll also be asked whether you (the representative) should keep access rights to manage the licence after issue.

My business is not yet registered. Can I apply for the Trading License directly?

No. You must first register the business with the Registrar General’s Department (and obtain a TIN with the GRA). If you’re a sole proprietor, the simplest path is to use the Register a Sole Proprietorship service, which handles all four registrations — including the KMC Trading License — in one application.

I’m a foreigner. Can I get a KMC Trading License?

Yes, provided you have a valid residence/work permit in The Gambia and the business is duly registered with the Business Registry. Select “Passport” as your document type.

Can a company apply, or only sole proprietorships?

Both. The form auto-detects the business category from the Business Registry. For companies, the system also verifies that the applicant is authorised to act on behalf of the company. If not, you’ll see a warning notice and won’t be able to proceed.

Can I declare multiple activities in the same licence?

Yes. The “Trading licence” grid on the License Details tab lets you add as many activity rows as needed. The KMC will price each one and combine them into a single invoice.

How is the fee calculated?

Each activity has a per-unit fee published in the KMC Trading Licenses Schedule. The system multiplies the per-unit fee by the count you declare (rooms, machines, vehicles, etc.). For retail activities, the fee may also depend on stock value bands. The KMC officer confirms the calculation during Review and Approval.

Can I see the fees before applying?

The exact fee is only confirmed by KMC after Review. The final amount appears on the invoice you receive after KMC Approval.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The KMC permits instalment payments. Each instalment is recorded against the same invoice, and the remaining balance updates after each payment. The licence is only issued once the balance reaches zero.

What payment methods are accepted?

Bank card and the digital payment methods integrated with Gambia Easy Business. The exact options are listed on the payment page.

What if I disagree with the calculated fee?

If the fee seems wrong, contact KMC before paying. You can also let the application sit at the Payment desk while you query the figures — just don’t leave it for too long, or the invoice may expire and the file may be cancelled.

Can I save my application and come back later?

Yes. The portal auto-saves your progress on each tab. Log out and back in any time — your draft is available from your dashboard.

My application was sent back for corrections. What now?

Log in, open the application from your dashboard. You’ll see the officer’s comments, including what to fix. Make the corrections and click “Submit” again — no need to pay again.

I paid but my certificate is still missing. What should I do?

Allow up to 30 minutes for the certificate generation bots to complete. If your status is “Bot failed” or no certificate appears after that, contact Gambia Easy Business support with your application reference number.

What browser should I use?

The portal works best on the latest versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari. Avoid Internet Explorer.
