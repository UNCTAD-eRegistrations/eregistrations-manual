Register a Trading License

Apply online for your KMC Trading License through the Kanifing Municipal Council. Submit your license activities, get your invoice, pay, and download your signed trading license certificate — all from your Gambia Easy Business account.

1. Service Overview

The Register a Trading License service on the Gambia Easy Business portal lets you apply online for a KMC Trading License — the annual operating licence required for businesses physically located within the Kanifing Municipal Council (KMC) jurisdiction (Serrekunda, Bakau, Fajara, Kanifing, and surrounding communities).

You complete one online form describing your business and the activities you carry out (e.g. how many rooms in your guesthouse, how many telecentre booths, how many trucks). The KMC reviews and approves your declaration, calculates the fee, issues an invoice you pay through the portal, and finally generates your signed Trading License Certificate — which you download from your dashboard.

What you receive at the end

A digitally generated KMC Trading License Certificate valid for one year, accessible from your Gambia Easy Business dashboard. You can re-download or print it at any time during its validity.

Annual renewal required

The KMC Trading License is valid for one calendar year. You must renew it annually to continue operating legally. The portal will notify you before your licence is due to expire.

2. Who Needs a Trading License

A KMC Trading License is required if all three of the following are true for your business:

  • Your business operates within the Kanifing Municipal Council area (Serrekunda, Bakau, Fajara, Kanifing, Bakoteh, Tallinding, Latrikunda, and other KMC communities).
  • Your business carries out a commercial activity covered by the KMC Trading Licenses Schedule (see categories below).
  • You operate from a physical premise, vehicle, machine, or asset based in KMC.

Activity categories covered

KMC calculates the fee based on your declared business activities. The schedule includes:

Per room
Accommodation

Hotels, guesthouses, lodges — fee charged per room.

Per plant / Per truck
Automobile Industry

Vehicle workshops, plants, transport fleets — fee per plant or per truck.

Per machine
Carpentry & Tradesmen

Carpenters, mechanics, and tradesmen with machinery — fee per machine.

Per booth
Communications

Telecentres and communication booths — fee per booth.

Per machine / Per show
Entertainment

Gaming machines, cinemas, event venues — fee per machine or per show.

Per premise
Financial Institutions

Banks, microfinance, foreign exchange — fee per business premise.

Per machine
Printing & Stationery

Print shops and stationery businesses — fee per printing machine.

Per boat / cold store
Sea Food Industry

Fishing boats and seafood cold storage — fee per boat or per cold store.

Per borehole
Utilities

Water boreholes and utility services — fee per borehole.

Per vehicle / billboard / m³
Other Businesses

Catch-all categories: per vehicle, per billboard, per cubic metre — covers other declared assets.

Don’t know your category?

The application form lets you pick a Sector first (e.g. “Accommodation facilities”) and then an Activity within that sector (e.g. “Per room”). Each activity automatically reveals only the relevant counter field — so if you pick “Accommodation — Per room” you’ll only see a “How many rooms?” field, not all 13 counters at once.

3. Before You Start

What You Need

  • A registered account on services.easybusiness.gov.gm
  • A valid identity document: National ID, Passport, or Driver’s License
  • Your business’s Business Registration Number and TIN (if the business is already registered with the Business Registry / GRA)
  • Your business’s physical address in the KMC area and a description of activities
  • An accurate count of premises / rooms / machines / vehicles / etc. — whatever applies to your activity
  • A bank card or digital payment method to pay the KMC fee after the invoice is issued

Where to start

This service handles the Trading License only. If your business is brand new and you need to register the business itself first, start with one of these services instead — they include the Trading License as part of a one-stop application:

  • Register a Sole Proprietorship — for individual-owned businesses (also creates BR, TIN, Employer Registration, and KMC Trading License in one go).
  • Register a Company — for limited liability companies operating in KMC.
Use this service when…

You’re an existing registered business applying for a KMC Trading License on its own — either because you’re a new entrant into the KMC jurisdiction, you’ve added a licensable activity, or you’re registering separately from your business registration.

Applying for yourself vs. for someone else

You can submit this application:

  • For yourself — you own and operate the business in your own name.
  • For someone else — you are an agent, manager, or representative acting on behalf of the business owner. In that case, you’ll be asked whether the representative will receive ongoing access rights to manage the licence after issue.

Estimated Processing Time

Phase Typical duration What happens
KMC Review 2–3 working days A reviewing officer checks your declared activities and proposes the licensing fee.
KMC Approval 1–2 working days A senior officer approves the fee, finalises the invoice, and issues it to you.
Payment Same day — depends on you You pay the invoice in full (or by instalments, where permitted). Processing continues only after full payment is confirmed.

5. Fees & How They’re Calculated

The Trading License fee is set by the KMC Trading Licenses Schedule. You don’t need to calculate it yourself — the system looks up your sector and activity, applies the multiplier (rooms, booths, machines, etc.), and shows you the total before you pay.

Fee structure

Each activity has a base fee per unit, multiplied by the number of units you declare. For example:

Sector Activity Counter declared Fee basis
AccommodationPer roomNumber of roomsPer-room schedule fee × rooms
AutomobilePer plantNumber of plantsPer-plant schedule fee × plants
AutomobilePer truckNumber of trucksPer-truck schedule fee × trucks
CarpentryPer machineNumber of machinesPer-machine schedule fee × machines
CommunicationsPer boothNumber of boothsPer-booth schedule fee × booths
EntertainmentPer machine / per showNumber of machines / number of showsSchedule fee × quantity
Financial InstitutionsPer premiseNumber of premisesPer-premise schedule fee × premises
Printing & StationeryPer machineNumber of machinesPer-machine schedule fee × machines
Sea foodPer boat / per cold storeNumber of boats / cold storesSchedule fee × quantity
TradesmenPer machineNumber of machinesPer-machine schedule fee × machines
UtilitiesPer boreholeNumber of boreholesPer-borehole schedule fee × boreholes
Other businessesPer vehicle / per billboard / per m³Number of vehicles / billboards / m³Schedule fee × quantity
Tip

You can declare multiple activities in one application (e.g. an accommodation business with rooms AND a communications booth on the same premise). The system adds them up into one total invoice. Each row in the “Trading licence” grid is one activity.

Stock value (for retail trade)

For certain retail categories, the fee depends on your declared stock value. The KMC officer enters this at the review stage based on the documentation you provide. There are three bands used in the schedule:

  • Stock value <= 100,000 GMD — lowest band
  • Stock value 100,001–200,000 GMD — mid band
  • Stock value > 200,000 GMD — highest band

Partial payments

The KMC permits payment by instalments. After the invoice is issued you can pay in one go or split the amount into multiple payments. Your licence is only finalised once the full invoice amount has been paid — partial payments are tracked on the portal until you reach the total.

Currency

All amounts are in Gambian Dalasi (GMD). The total cost is shown clearly on the invoice you receive after KMC Approval.

    Review & submit

    The portal shows a final summary of everything you entered: owner details, business details, declared activities, and uploaded documents. Read it carefully — this is the version that goes to KMC.

    • Use the Edit links to go back and correct any field.
    • Tick the declaration checkbox confirming the information is true.
    • Click Submit application to send the file to KMC Review.
    No payment at submission

    Unlike other services, you do not pay at the time of submission. KMC must first review your declaration and issue an invoice. You’ll be invited to pay later, from your dashboard, once KMC Approval is done.

    Wait for the KMC invoice

    Your file goes through two officer desks at KMC: KMC Review (a reviewing officer checks the declaration and proposes the fee) and KMC Approval (a senior officer validates the fee and issues the invoice).

    When the invoice is ready, you receive a notification by email and on your dashboard. Open the application and you’ll see:

    • The Invoice N°
    • The Total KMC fees due (in GMD)
    • A “Print invoice” button
    • A payment due date

    Pay the invoice

    Pay through the portal using your preferred payment method. You can pay the full amount in one go, or by instalments: each payment is recorded against the same invoice and you can see the “remaining to be paid” balance after each instalment.

    The licence is only generated once the full invoice amount has been paid. While there’s an outstanding balance, the file stays at the KMC Payment desk. Once the balance reaches zero, the file automatically advances to certificate generation.

    After the final payment, the KMC Registration bot writes your licence into the KMC Trading Licenses database and the KMC Certificate bot generates a signed PDF certificate. You receive a notification, and the certificate appears on your application dashboard with an “Issued” status.

    • See your Licence Number, Issued date, and Renewal date

7. After Submission

Once your application is submitted, it moves through six desks — three reviewed by humans (KMC officers), two handled by automation bots, and one back to you to pay. You can follow the status of your file on your dashboard at any time.

Processing Workflow

KMC Review

A KMC reviewing officer opens your file, checks your declared activities against records, looks up the appropriate licensing fees in the schedule, and validates your application. They may also enter inspection details (date of inspection, zone, etc.) and a recommended stock value if your activity is retail-based.

2–3 working days — status: FILE PENDING then FILE VALIDATED

KMC Approval

A senior officer reviews the reviewer’s work, confirms the total cost, sets “Issue invoice” to Yes, and finalises the invoice number. The system generates a printable invoice. The officer may also send back to KMC Review for corrections.

1–2 working days — status: FILE VALIDATED → KMC Payment

You: pay the invoice

The portal notifies you that the invoice is ready. You log in, see the amount and due date, and pay through the portal — in one or several instalments. Each payment is tracked on the application page.

As soon as you’re ready — status: FILE PENDING at KMC Payment

KMC Payment confirmation

The Payment officer confirms each instalment as it arrives. When the remaining balance reaches zero, the officer validates the application and it moves on automatically.

Same day after each instalment — status: FILE VALIDATED on full payment

KMC Registration (automated)

An automation bot writes the licence into the KMC Trading Licenses database, creates the relations between you (or the representative) and the licence in the rights register, and links payments to the new licence number.

Automatic, seconds after full payment — bot-driven

KMC Certificate generation (automated)

A document bot generates the signed Trading License Certificate PDF using your data and the KMC template, then uploads it to your dashboard as a downloadable file.

Automatic, seconds after the previous step

Completion — certificate available

Your KMC Trading License certificate is now available from your dashboard. You receive an email notification and the application status shows as Issued/Validated.

Final state — renew next year

8. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a KMC Trading License?

It’s the annual operating licence required by the Kanifing Municipal Council for any business that carries out a commercial activity inside KMC. Without it, KMC inspectors can shut down your operation or fine you for trading without a licence.

How long is the licence valid?

The Trading License is valid for one year from the date of issue. You must renew it each year before the renewal date shown on your certificate.

Can I apply if my business is outside the KMC area?

No. This service is for businesses physically located within KMC. If your business is in Banjul, Brikama, or another council, contact your local municipal authority — KMC has no jurisdiction over you.

Can I apply on behalf of someone else?

Yes. Choose “Someone else” on the License Owner tab and enter the owner’s details. You’ll also be asked whether you (the representative) should keep access rights to manage the licence after issue.

My business is not yet registered. Can I apply for the Trading License directly?

No. You must first register the business with the Registrar General’s Department (and obtain a TIN with the GRA). If you’re a sole proprietor, the simplest path is to use the Register a Sole Proprietorship service, which handles all four registrations — including the KMC Trading License — in one application.

I’m a foreigner. Can I get a KMC Trading License?

Yes, provided you have a valid residence/work permit in The Gambia and the business is duly registered with the Business Registry. Select “Passport” as your document type.

Can a company apply, or only sole proprietorships?

Both. The form auto-detects the business category from the Business Registry. For companies, the system also verifies that the applicant is authorised to act on behalf of the company. If not, you’ll see a warning notice and won’t be able to proceed.

Can I declare multiple activities in the same licence?

Yes. The “Trading licence” grid on the License Details tab lets you add as many activity rows as needed. The KMC will price each one and combine them into a single invoice.

How is the fee calculated?

Each activity has a per-unit fee published in the KMC Trading Licenses Schedule. The system multiplies the per-unit fee by the count you declare (rooms, machines, vehicles, etc.). For retail activities, the fee may also depend on stock value bands. The KMC officer confirms the calculation during Review and Approval.

Can I see the fees before applying?

The exact fee is only confirmed by KMC after Review. However, the portal will display the indicative fee based on the schedule once you complete your activity list. The final amount appears on the invoice you receive after KMC Approval.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The KMC permits instalment payments. Each instalment is recorded against the same invoice, and the remaining balance updates after each payment. The licence is only issued once the balance reaches zero.

What payment methods are accepted?

Bank card and the digital payment methods integrated with Gambia Easy Business. The exact options are listed on the payment page.

What if I disagree with the calculated fee?

If the fee seems wrong, contact KMC before paying. You can also let the application sit at the Payment desk while you query the figures — just don’t leave it for too long, or the invoice may expire and the file may be cancelled.

Can I save my application and come back later?

Yes. The portal auto-saves your progress on each tab. Log out and back in any time — your draft is available from your dashboard.

My application was sent back for corrections. What now?

Log in, open the application from your dashboard. You’ll see the comments the officer wrote, including what to fix. Make the corrections and click “Submit” again — no need to pay again.

I paid but my certificate is still missing. What should I do?

Allow up to 30 minutes for the certificate generation bots to complete. If your status is “Bot failed” or no certificate appears after that, contact Gambia Easy Business support with your application reference number.

What browser should I use?

The portal works best on the latest versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari. Avoid Internet Explorer.
