Register a Sole Proprietorship

Register your sole proprietorship in The Gambia through a single online application — covering your Business Registration, Tax Identification Number, Employer Registration, and KMC Trading License all at once.

1. Service Overview

The Register a Sole Proprietorship service on the Gambia Easy Business portal is a one-stop registration that processes four government registrations in a single online application. Instead of visiting multiple agencies and filling in separate forms, you complete everything in one place and pay once.

A sole proprietorship is the simplest business structure in The Gambia — a business owned and operated by one individual, with no legal distinction between the owner and the business.

One application, four registrations

Your single application simultaneously covers your Business Registration, Tax Identification Number (TIN), Employer Registration, and — if your business operates in the Kanifing Municipal Council area — your KMC Trading License. All results are delivered to your eRegistrations account.

One sole proprietorship per person

Gambian law allows a maximum of one sole proprietorship per individual. If you already have a sole proprietorship registered in The Gambia, the system will detect this and prevent a duplicate registration. If you need to register a second business, consider registering a company instead.

How the Service Works

When you log in and open the service, the system pre-fills any information already linked to your account (name, ID details, existing TIN, etc.). You then complete the remaining details about your business, review the fees, pay, and submit. The government processes your application and issues your registration certificates — all accessible from your eRegistrations dashboard.

2. Before You Start

What You Need

  • A registered account on services.easybusiness.gov.gm
  • A valid proof of identity: National ID, Passport, or Driver’s License
  • Your business address and a description of your planned business activities
  • A computer or smartphone with internet access
  • A valid payment method for the applicable registration fees
Optional: reserve a business name first

If you wish to operate under a business name other than your own full name, you must first reserve a business name through the Name Reservation service. Once reserved, you can select your reserved name when completing this application. If you are happy to trade under your own full name, no reservation is needed.

3. What’s Included (4 Registrations)

Your application simultaneously processes up to four registrations from four different government bodies. Use the tabs below to understand each one.

Business Registration

Issued by: Registrar General’s Department (RGD), Ministry of Justice

This is the core registration that legally establishes your sole proprietorship in The Gambia. Upon approval, you receive a Certificate of Business Registration and an official Business Registration Number.

The Registrar’s office will review your application, check that your business name is available (if you chose one), and verify your identity. If the name is similar to an existing business, the officer may contact you or request a name change before approving.

Business name check

The system automatically checks whether your proposed business name is already registered. If it conflicts with an existing name, you will be notified during the application and asked to provide an alternative.

Tax Identification Number (TIN)

Issued by: Gambia Revenue Authority (GRA)

Every business operating in The Gambia is required to have a Tax Identification Number. The TIN is used for all tax-related interactions with the Gambia Revenue Authority, including filing returns and paying taxes.

If you already have a personal TIN registered with the GRA, the system will detect this and link it to your new business automatically. If you do not yet have a TIN, one will be generated for you as part of this application.

Upon completion, a TIN Card is generated and made available for download from your eRegistrations dashboard.

Employer Registration

Issued by: Social Security and Housing Finance Corporation (SSHFC)

If your sole proprietorship will employ at least one person, you are required to register as an employer with the SSHFC. This registration ensures your employees are covered by the Social Security scheme.

The system includes Employer Registration automatically in your application. If you indicate during the application that you have no employees, this registration step will be completed with zero employees recorded and can be updated later when you hire staff.

Update later

You can update your number of employees at any time after registration through the Update Information for Sole Proprietorship service on the portal.

KMC Trading License

Issued by: Kanifing Municipal Council (KMC)

If your business is physically located within the Kanifing Municipal Council area (Serrekunda, Bakau, Fajara, Kanifing, and surrounding communities), you are required to obtain a KMC Trading License to operate legally.

The system automatically determines whether your business address falls within the KMC jurisdiction. If it does, the KMC Trading License registration is included in your application. If your business is located outside the KMC area, this step is skipped.

The KMC Trading License fee is calculated based on your business type and activity (e.g., accommodation per room, retail trade per outlet, food service, communications, etc.). The fee is displayed clearly before payment.

Annual renewal required

The KMC Trading License must be renewed annually. You will be notified through the portal when your license is approaching its expiry date.

4. Requirements & Documents

Identity Documents

You must provide one of the following as proof of identity. The document type you select determines which document number field is shown in the form.

Document Who Can Use It Notes
National ID Gambian citizens Recommended for Gambian nationals. The system cross-checks your ID number against existing records.
Passport All applicants Accepted for both nationals and foreign residents.
Driver’s License All applicants Accepted as a secondary identity document.

Account & Business Information Required

  • Your full name (first, middle if applicable, and last name) as it appears on your ID
  • Your date of birth
  • Your nationality and residential address
  • Your business name (your own full name, or a registered/reserved business name)
  • A description of your business activities
  • Your business address (determines KMC license requirement)
Applying on behalf of someone else

You may complete this application on behalf of another person (e.g., as an agent or representative). In that case, select "Application is for someone else" at the start of the form and enter the business owner’s details. You will be asked to specify whether the representative will have full or limited access rights to the application.

5. Fees

All fees are calculated automatically by the system based on your business type, location, and activities. The exact amounts are displayed in the application before you proceed to payment. All amounts are in Gambian Dalasi (GMD).

Fee Structure by Registration

Registration Fee Basis Notes
Business Registration Fixed registration fee Standard government fee for sole proprietorship registration at the Registrar General’s Department
Tax Identification Number No fee TIN registration is free of charge
Employer Registration Fixed registration fee Standard SSHFC employer registration fee
KMC Trading License Variable — based on business type Fee varies by category: accommodation (per room), retail trade, food service, communications (per booth), automobile industry (per plant/truck), and others. Calculated from your declared business activities.
Single payment

All applicable fees from all registrations are combined into a single payment. You will see an itemised fee breakdown before paying, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

KMC licence fee example

Amie runs a small guesthouse in Serrekunda with 6 rooms, and also operates a communications booth. The KMC Trading License fee would be calculated as: accommodation fee × 6 rooms + communications fee × 1 booth. Both are shown itemised in the fee summary before payment.

6. Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these steps to complete your sole proprietorship registration on services.easybusiness.gov.gm:

  1. 1

    Log In to Your Account

    Go to services.easybusiness.gov.gm and log in with your email and password. If you do not yet have an account, click “Register” to create one.

    New user?

    When creating your account, use the same details as on your identity document. The system will cross-check your name and ID number during the registration process.

  2. 2

    Open the Service

    From your dashboard, find and click the “Register a Sole Proprietorship” service card. The form opens and any information already linked to your account is pre-filled automatically.

    Already registered?

    If the system detects that you already have an active sole proprietorship in The Gambia, the application will inform you and prevent submission. Each person may hold only one sole proprietorship.

  3. 3

    Select Who You Are Applying For

    The first question asks whether the application is for yourself or someone else.

    If you select “Someone else”, you will be asked to enter the business owner’s personal details and specify the level of access rights the representative will have.

    Business Address & Activities

  6. 6

    Review Included Registrations & Fees

    After completing the form, the system displays a summary of all the registrations that will be processed and the associated fees. Review this carefully.

    • All applicable registrations are listed with their issuing authority
    • Fees are itemised per registration
    • The KMC Trading License section shows the fee breakdown by business activity type (only if your address is in the KMC area)
    • A total amount due is shown at the bottom
  7. 7

    Accept the Declaration & Proceed to Payment

    Before submitting, you must tick the declaration checkbox confirming that the information provided is true and correct to the best of your knowledge. You acknowledge that providing false information may lead to prosecution.

    Click “Proceed to Payment” to go to the payment page. Payment can be made by bank card or other available digital payment methods.

    Payment required before processing

    Your application is only sent for government review after successful payment. An incomplete payment will leave your application in a pending state — do not close your browser during the payment process.

  8. 8

    Submit & Receive Confirmation

    After successful payment, your application is automatically submitted to the relevant government bodies for review. You will receive:

    • An on-screen confirmation with your application reference number
    • A confirmation email summarising what was submitted

    Keep your reference number. You can use it to track the status of your application by logging back into the portal and opening your application from your dashboard.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to register a sole proprietorship in The Gambia?

Any individual — Gambian national or foreign resident with a valid permit to work in The Gambia — may register a sole proprietorship. Each person is limited to one sole proprietorship at a time.

Do I need to visit any government office?

No. The entire process — from application to certificate delivery — is handled online through the Gambia Easy Business portal. You do not need to visit the Registrar General’s Department, GRA, SSHFC, or KMC offices unless specifically requested by an officer.

Can a foreigner register a sole proprietorship?

Yes. Foreign nationals may register a sole proprietorship. The system supports non-Gambian applicants and accepts Passport as the identity document. Foreigners must have a valid residence/work permit in The Gambia.

What happens if my application is sent back for corrections?

Log in to the portal, open your application, and look for the officer’s comments explaining what needs to be corrected. Make the changes and resubmit. You do not need to pay again for a resubmission after corrections.

Do I have to register a business name?

No. You can operate under your own full name at no additional cost. Registering a distinct business name is optional but recommended if you want to trade under a brand name that is different from your personal name.

How do I reserve a business name before applying?

Use the Reserve a Business Name service on the Gambia Easy Business portal. Once your reservation is approved, return to this service and select your reserved name in the business name section of the form.

What if my proposed business name is rejected?

The officer reviewing your application will indicate the reason for rejection (e.g., name already in use, name is misleading or offensive). The application will be sent back to you for corrections, and you can propose a different name without restarting the entire process.

Can I check name availability before submitting?

Yes. When you enter a proposed business name in the form, a “Check Availability” button allows you to verify the name against the existing business register before submitting your application.

How do I know if my business needs a KMC Trading License?

The system determines this automatically based on the business address you enter. If your address falls within the Kanifing Municipal Council jurisdiction (including Serrekunda, Bakau, Fajara, Kanifing, and surrounding areas), the KMC license is included in your application.

What is the KMC Trading License fee based on?

The fee is calculated according to your business type and scale, using KMC’s published tariff schedule. Examples include: accommodation businesses (fee per room), automobile industry (fee per plant or per truck), carpentry (fee per machine), communications (fee per booth), and other categories. The exact amount is displayed in the fee summary before you pay.

My business is not in Kanifing — do I need a trading license?

For businesses outside the KMC area, a KMC Trading License is not required through this portal. Other local authorities may have their own licensing requirements — check with your local council.

When does my KMC Trading License expire?

KMC Trading Licenses are valid for one year from the date of issue. You will receive a notification through the portal when your license is approaching its expiry date, and you can renew it through the relevant renewal service.

Can I save my application and come back later?

Yes. The portal auto-saves your progress as you complete each section. You can log out and return to your in-progress application at any time from your dashboard.

My payment went through but my application status has not updated. What should I do?

Payment confirmation can sometimes take a few minutes to reflect in the system. If your application still shows “pending payment” after 30 minutes, contact the Gambia Easy Business help desk with your payment reference number.

What browser should I use?

The portal works best on the latest versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari. Avoid using Internet Explorer.

Can I download my certificates after they are issued?

Yes. All issued certificates and registration documents are stored in your eRegistrations account and can be downloaded at any time from your dashboard, even months after issuance.
