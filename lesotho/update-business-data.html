User Manual

Update Your Business Data

Keep your registered business information up to date — online, without visiting a government office. Whether you are a sole proprietor or a company, this service lets you modify your business name, activities, addresses, and more through the OBFC eRegistrations platform.

1. Overview

The Update Your Business Data service allows registered businesses in Lesotho to submit modification requests through the One Business Facilitation Centre (OBFC) online platform. Instead of visiting government offices in person, you can update your official registration information from anywhere, at any time.

This service handles a single registration type — Modification — and is available to both sole proprietors and incorporated companies. The form is dynamic: it detects your business type and only shows the sections that apply to you.

Who can use this service

Any business owner or authorised representative of a business or company registered in Lesotho. You must have an active account on the OBFC eRegistrations platform and know your Business ID.

Note

You do not need to update all categories at once. Select only the specific areas you wish to modify. Each category has its own independent form section and confirmation step.

Sole Proprietorship vs. Company

The modifications available to you depend on your business type:

  • Sole Proprietorship — You can update your business name, activities, addresses, and turnover/employee information. Capital, shareholders, managers, and company type sections do not apply.
  • Company (Pty Ltd or other) — You have access to all modification categories, including capital structure, shareholders, managers, and company type in addition to the sections available to sole proprietors.

2. What Can Be Updated

Select your business type below to see which data categories you can modify.

As a sole proprietor, you can modify the following categories by ticking the relevant checkboxes when you start the modification request:

CategoryWhat You Can Change
Business Name Change the registered trading name of your business. A name availability check is performed automatically.
Business Names (per Activity) Update or add a trading name associated with a specific business activity.
Activities Add new business activities (by sector) or close (discontinue) existing ones. Licensing fees may apply for new activities.
Main Office Update the address, district, town, telephone, and email of your principal place of business.
Branches (Operating Offices) Add, modify, or remove branch office locations with full contact details.

Capital, shareholders, managers, and company type sections are not available for sole proprietorships and will not appear in your form.

3. Requirements

Make sure you have everything ready before you start. The items you need depend on your business type and the categories you wish to modify.

Account & Access

  • An active account on the OBFC eRegistrations platform (services.businessregistrations.gov.ls)
  • Your Business ID (shown on your dashboard and on your registration certificate)
  • A device with internet access (computer, tablet, or smartphone)

Documents

For most sole proprietorship modifications, no supporting documents are required. The system already holds your personal details from the national identity database.

However, if you are adding a new business activity that requires a trading licence, you may need to provide activity-specific information (such as the proposed trading name and sector).

Tip

Before starting, note down the exact changes you want to make — for example, the new business name, new activity sector, or updated office address — so you can fill in the form without interruption.

7. After Submission

Once you submit your modification request, it enters a structured review and approval workflow. Both sole proprietorships and companies follow the same process.

Application Submitted

Your modification request is received by OBFC. You receive a reference number and confirmation by email. Track progress from your dashboard.

Day 0

Review (OBFC Officer)

An OBFC review officer checks the application for completeness and accuracy — verifying documents, checking name availability results, and validating the modifications.

Day 1–3

Approval (Senior Officer)

A senior OBFC officer reviews the validated application and either approves it or sends it back for corrections. If sent back, you will be notified by email with the officer's comments.

Day 3–5

Automatic Processing

Once approved, the system automatically updates your registration data — business name, activities, addresses, shareholders, managers, and all linked records.

Day 5–6

Updated Certificate Issued

An updated business registration certificate reflecting your changes is generated and available for download from your dashboard.

Day 6–7

Possible Outcomes

StatusWhat It MeansWhat to Do
File Validated Your application passed the review stage No action needed — it proceeds to approval
File Pending Additional review or information is required Check your email for messages from the reviewing officer
Sent Back for Corrections Issues were found and your application has been returned Log in, open the returned application, correct the flagged items, and resubmit
Approved Your modifications have been accepted and applied Download your updated certificate from the dashboard
Rejected The modification request was denied Review the rejection reason, correct the issue, and submit a new request
Track your application

You can monitor the status of your modification request at any time by logging into your account and checking your application history on the dashboard.

8. FAQ & Contact

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I update multiple categories at the same time?

Yes. When you start the modification request, you can tick as many categories as needed. Each selected category will have its own dedicated form section within the same application.

How do I know if a proposed business name is available?

After entering your proposed name, click the Check Availability button. The system instantly searches the OBFC registry and tells you if the name is available or already in use. If taken, you can enter a different name and check again.

What happens if my application is sent back for corrections?

You will receive an email notification. Log in to your account, open the returned application, review the officer's comments (shown alongside the relevant fields), make the necessary corrections, and resubmit. Your application will then re-enter the review queue.

Can I cancel a modification request after submission?

Once submitted, you cannot cancel or edit the request directly through the platform. Contact the OBFC helpline for assistance if you need to withdraw a pending application.

What file formats are accepted for document uploads?

The system accepts PDF, JPG, and PNG files. Each file must be no larger than 5 MB. Make sure documents are legible, not password-protected, and clearly show all required information.

How long does the process take?

Most modification requests are processed within 5 to 7 working days, provided all information is complete and accurate. Applications that require corrections will take longer depending on how quickly corrections are submitted.

Is there a fee for address-only changes?

No. Updating your main office address or branch addresses does not incur any fee for either sole proprietorships or companies.

Do I need to update shareholders if I change the capital?

If the capital change affects individual shareholdings (e.g., new shares are issued or the value per share changes), you should also update the Shareholders section. The system validates that the total declared value of all shareholders' shares equals the modified capital. If they do not match, you cannot submit.

Can I add a legal entity (company) as a shareholder?

Yes. When adding a new shareholder, select "Legal entity (company/corporate)" as the shareholder type. You will be asked for the company name, country of incorporation, company type, and incorporation details. Upload the relevant corporate registration document.

What is the difference between "fixed" and "open-ended" mandate for managers?

A fixed mandate means the manager's appointment is for a defined term (e.g., 2 years). An open-ended mandate means the appointment has no fixed end date and continues until the manager is removed or resigns.

Can I change from a Private Company to a Public Company?

Yes, through the "Company Type" modification category. Be aware that changing company type may have legal and regulatory implications beyond this modification. Consult a legal adviser if needed.

Can I add shareholders as a sole proprietor?

No. The shareholder, manager, and capital sections are only available for incorporated companies. If you want to bring in partners or investors with formal ownership rights, you would need to convert your sole proprietorship into a company — a separate process.

Do I need to upload any documents to update my sole proprietorship?

For most modifications (name, address, activities, turnover), no documents are required. Your personal details are already on record from the national identity database. If you add a new activity requiring a licence, you may need to provide activity-specific details, but no document uploads are needed.

Can I change my sole proprietorship to a company using this service?

No. Converting from a sole proprietorship to a company is a different procedure. Please contact OBFC support for guidance on business conversion.
