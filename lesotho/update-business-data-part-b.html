Update Your Business Data

Back-office processing guide for OBFC officers. Covers the full review and approval workflow for business modification requests submitted by sole proprietors and companies, including the automated bots that update the registry and generate updated certificates.

1. Service Overview

The Update Your Business Data service allows registered businesses in Lesotho to submit modification requests online through the OBFC eRegistrations portal. There is one registration type: Modification.

Both sole proprietorships and companies use the same service. The applicant selects the data categories they wish to modify, fills in the relevant sections, and submits. The application then enters the officer review queue.

Business TypeCategories available for modification
Sole Proprietorship Name, Business names per activity, Activities, Main office, Branches, Turnover & employees
Company Name, Business names per activity, Activities, Main office, Branches, Capital, Shareholders, Managers, Company type, Turnover & employees
Same workflow, different checklists

Both sole proprietorships and companies go through the same Review Desk → Approval Desk → Bot sequence. The difference is in what the officer checks: sole proprietorship files have no document uploads to review, while company files may include ID documents for new shareholders or managers.

Full Timeline

Applicant Submits

The citizen completes the modification form online, selecting which data categories to update, filling in the proposed changes, confirming each section, and submitting. If licensing fees apply (e.g. new activity), payment is made online before or at submission. The application enters the back-office queue.

Triggered by citizen

Review Desk

A reviewing officer opens the application and checks each selected modification category for completeness, accuracy, and validity. For company files with new shareholders or managers, the officer also verifies uploaded identity documents. The officer can validate, send back for corrections, or reject.

Human officer — typically 1–3 working days

Approval Desk

A senior officer gives final sign-off on the reviewed application. Verifies that the Review Desk has correctly checked all modification categories. Once approved (FILE VALIDATED), the bots trigger automatically.

Human officer — typically 1–2 working days after review

MODIFICATION Update Bot

Automatically updates the business or company record in the OBFC national business registry with all approved changes: name, activities, addresses, capital structure, shareholders, managers, company type, and related records.

Automated — runs immediately after Approval Desk validates

MODIFICATION Upload Bot

Generates an updated registration certificate PDF reflecting all approved modifications and uploads it to the citizen's eRegistrations account. The citizen is notified by email that their updated certificate is available for download.

Automated — runs after MODIFICATION Update succeeds

3. Type of Roles

There are two types of roles in an eRegistrations service:

Assigned to a government officer who manually reviews or approves applications. The officer sees the full modification form, the proposed changes for each category, uploaded documents, and payment confirmation. They can add comments and set a status to move the file forward or return it to the applicant.

Role Statuses

StatusMeaning
FILE PENDING Application is queued or in progress at this desk. No action required unless it has been stalled for more than 24 hours.
FILE VALIDATED Desk approved the application. It moves automatically to the next step in the workflow.
SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS Application returned to the applicant with officer comments. The applicant must correct the flagged issue and resubmit.
FILE REJECT Application permanently rejected. No further processing. The applicant is notified and must submit a new modification request.

Executed automatically by the system — no officer action required. Bots run in sequence after the previous step succeeds. They update the registry database and generate the updated certificate PDF.

Bot Statuses

StatusMeaningAction Required
FILE VALIDATED Bot ran successfully None — proceeds to next step automatically
FILE PENDING Bot is queued or currently running Wait; check again after a few minutes
Bot failed Bot encountered an error during execution See troubleshooting steps in Section 5

Troubleshooting: Bot Failed

  1. 1

    Check the bot error log

    Open the application in the back-office portal and navigate to the failed bot step. Click the error details icon to see the specific failure reason and error message.

  2. 2

    Common failure reasons

    • Business ID not found in registry — The business record may have been corrupted or removed. Contact the database administrator.
    • Capital mismatch — Total value of shares does not equal declared capital in the registry. May require sending back to corrections.
    • Database connection timeout — Retry the bot; usually resolves on its own after a few minutes.
    • Mapping error — A field mapping may be outdated. Contact the system administrator.
    • Name conflict detected post-approval — A concurrent application may have registered the same business name. Contact the supervisor.
  3. 3

    Retry the bot

    If the error is transient (e.g. connection issue), use the Retry action in the bot step. If the error persists after two retries, escalate to the system administrator with the full error log.

4. Description of Roles

  1. 1

    Review Desk

    The reviewing officer opens the modification application and verifies each selected category for completeness, accuracy, and eligibility. The checklist differs by business type:

    General

    • Business type confirmed as Sole Proprietorship
    • Business ID matches the registered entity in the system

    If "Name" was selected

    • Proposed business name is filled in and availability check was performed
    • Name is not already registered by another entity
    • Applicant confirmed the name modification

    If "Activities" was selected

    • New activities have a valid sector and activity type selected
    • Licensing fees are correctly calculated by the system for licensed activities
    • Activities marked for closure are existing registered activities

    If "Main Office" or "Branches" was selected

    • Address, district, and town fields are filled and valid
    No document uploads

    Sole proprietorship modification files do not include document uploads. No identity or corporate documents need to be checked.

    General

    • Business type confirmed as Company
    • Business ID matches the registered company in the system

    If "Name" was selected

    • Proposed company name filled, availability check performed, name is available

    If "Activities" was selected

    • New activities have valid sector and activity type; licensing fees correctly calculated

    If "Capital" was selected

    • Modified capital amount is filled
    • Number of ordinary shares × value per share = declared capital (system validates this)
    • Preferred share classes, if present, are complete (class name, number, value)

    If "Shareholders" was selected

    • Personal details complete for each new/modified natural person shareholder
    • ID Document uploaded for each new/modified shareholder — legible, not expired
    • For legal entity shareholders: corporate registration document uploaded
    • Total value of final shareholders' holdings equals the declared capital

    If "Managers" was selected

    • Personal details complete for each new/modified manager
    • ID Document uploaded for each new/modified manager — legible, not expired
    • Role and mandate type assigned to each manager

    If "Company Type" was selected

    • New company type selected and is a valid conversion from the current type

    If anything is missing or incorrect, use SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS with a clear note specifying which modification category has an issue and what needs to be corrected. Only use FILE REJECT for cases that cannot be corrected (e.g. fraudulent documents or modification not permitted by law).

  2. 2

    Approval Desk

    The approving officer performs a final check on the reviewed application before giving sign-off. This is typically a senior officer or supervisor. The approver confirms that:

    • The Review Desk officer has correctly checked all selected modification categories
    • All documents are present and verified (company files)
    • The proposed modifications are lawful and consistent with the company's registered information
    • Any fees have been paid in full

    If satisfied, set the status to FILE VALIDATED. This triggers the MODIFICATION Update bot immediately. If there are concerns, use FILE REJECT with a written justification, or return to the Review Desk for re-examination.

  3. 3

    MODIFICATION Update Bot

    This bot runs automatically after the Approval Desk validates the file. It applies all approved modifications to the business or company record in the OBFC national registry database:

    • Name: Updates the business/company name in the registry
    • Activities: Adds new activities, removes discontinued ones, updates trading names per activity
    • Main Office / Branches: Updates address, district, town, and contact details
    • Capital: Updates declared capital, share structure, and preferred share classes
    • Shareholders: Adds new shareholders, modifies or removes existing ones with updated shareholdings
    • Managers: Adds new managers, modifies or removes existing ones with updated roles
    • Company Type: Updates the legal classification of the company

    No officer action is required. Monitor the status in the back-office portal. If the bot fails, see troubleshooting steps in Section 3 or Section 5.

  4. 4

    MODIFICATION Upload Bot

    Once the MODIFICATION Update bot completes successfully, this bot generates an updated registration certificate PDF and uploads it to the citizen's eRegistrations account dashboard. The certificate reflects all approved modifications.

    The citizen receives an email notification that the updated certificate is ready for download. No officer action is required. If this bot fails after the Update bot has succeeded, retry first before escalating.

5. Assistance

Common Issues

IssueLikely CauseRecommended Action
Proposed business name shown as available in form but now conflicts with another registration A concurrent application registered the same name between the applicant's check and the review Send back to corrections asking the applicant to propose a different name and run the availability check again
Capital declared does not match total of shareholders' holdings Applicant modified capital without updating shareholders, or entered incorrect share values Send back to corrections specifying that the capital total must equal the sum of all shareholders' share values
Uploaded ID document is unreadable, expired, or incorrect file Poor scan quality, wrong file, or expired document uploaded Send back to corrections specifying which document (shareholder or manager) needs to be re-uploaded and why
Applicant selected an activity that does not match the company's permitted sectors Incorrect sector or activity type selected Send back to corrections with a note identifying the incorrect activity and the correct sector to use
Payment confirmed but licensing fee shows as unpaid Payment gateway delay Wait up to 1 hour for the payment to synchronise. If still unresolved, verify payment with the finance team and confirm manually if appropriate.
MODIFICATION Update bot failed — "Business ID not found" Business record may have been corrupted or the Business ID was incorrectly retrieved Contact the database administrator to verify the record. Do not retry without confirmation.
MODIFICATION Update bot failed — "Capital mismatch" The capital and shareholders' share values do not reconcile in the registry Review the capital and shareholders sections; send back to corrections if a data inconsistency exists
MODIFICATION Upload bot failed — certificate not generated Template error or missing data from the Update bot Confirm the Update bot completed successfully first. Then retry the Upload bot. If it still fails, contact IT Support with the full error log.
