User Manual — Applicant

File Annual Report

Submit your company’s annual financial statements and keep your company records up to date with the Companies Registry through the OBFC eRegistrations platform.

1. Overview

The File Annual Report service allows registered companies in the Kingdom of Lesotho to fulfil their annual compliance obligations electronically through the One Business Facilitation Centre (OBFC) eRegistrations platform. It replaces the need to visit government offices in person.

This service covers two registrations:

  • Annual Filing — Submit your company’s financial statements for each fiscal year due.
  • Update of Data — Update your company’s contact information, address, and other details in the Companies Registry without submitting a full annual filing.
Key Benefits

File online 24/7 without visiting any office. Track the status of your filings in real time and file for multiple years in a single session.

Processing Workflow

Once submitted, your annual filing goes through the following steps:

Submission

You submit your annual filing online with the required financial statements attached.

Officer Review

An OBFC officer reviews your submission and verifies the uploaded financial statements. The officer may approve or return the filing for corrections.

Register in Companies Database

Once approved, the filing is automatically registered in the Companies database (automated process).

Register in Accounts Database

The filing is also registered in the Accounts database (automated process). Your annual report obligation for that year is now fulfilled.

2. Who Must File

All companies registered with the Lesotho Companies Registry are required to file annual reports. This obligation applies to:

Private companies (Pty Ltd)
Public companies (Ltd)
External (foreign) companies operating in Lesotho
Any other entity registered under the Companies Act, 2011
Important

You must be a registered user on the eRegistrations platform and linked to your company to submit an annual report. If you do not yet have an account, create one first at services.businessregistrations.gov.ls.

3. Requirements

Before starting your filing, ensure you have the following ready.

5. Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these steps to complete your annual report online.

Step 1

Log In to the Platform

Visit services.businessregistrations.gov.ls and log in with your registered email and password. If you do not have an account, register first and ensure your profile is linked to your company.

Step 2

Select the File Annual Report Service

From your dashboard or the services catalogue, locate and select the File Annual Report service. The system will automatically load your company information and display any pending or completed filings.

Step 3

Review Your Company Information

The Company Information tab displays your company’s current details from the registry, including:

  • Company name, registration number, and legal type
  • Capital amount and expiration date
  • Business sector and activities
  • List of owners with names and ID numbers
  • Contact details (email, phone) and address (district, town, street)
Update of Data

To update contact or address information only (without filing financial statements), modify the relevant fields on this tab and click “Update contact information”. This submits an Update of Data registration without requiring a financial statement.

Step 4

Proceed to Annual Filings Tab

Click “Proceed to annual filing” to navigate to the Annual Filings tab. You will see two sub-tabs:

  • Filings Due — All pending filings for your company, with the year and due date for each.
  • Filings Completed — Previously submitted filings with their filing dates and uploaded statements.
Step 5

Upload Financial Statements

For each year shown in the Filings Due tab:

  1. Tick the “Upload statements” checkbox next to the relevant year.
  2. Click the file upload field and select your company’s financial statements (PDF recommended).
  3. Repeat for each year you wish to file.
Important

The Financial Statement upload is required for each year you are filing. You cannot submit the Annual Filing registration without attaching a document.

Step 6

Confirm and Submit

Review your information and tick the confirmation checkbox (“I confirm the company details”). Click “File your annual statements” to submit. The system validates your form before final submission.

Step 7

Track Your Filing

After submission, track the status of your filing through your dashboard. You will be notified if the reviewing officer requires corrections or additional information.

7. Deadlines & Penalties

Companies are required to file their annual returns by the due date assigned by the Companies Registry. Key points:

Aspect Details
Filing frequency Annual — once per fiscal year per company.
Due date As shown in your Filings Due grid. Due dates are set by the Companies Registry based on your company’s registration date.
Late filing Companies that fail to file by the due date may be subject to penalties under the Companies Act, 2011. Persistent non-compliance may result in the company being struck off the register.
Multiple years outstanding If you have filings due for several years, you can submit them all in one session. Each year requires its own financial statements upload.
Warning

Failure to file annual reports can lead to your company being deregistered. File on time every year to maintain your company’s good standing with the registry.

8. After Submission

Once you have submitted, here is what happens next:

1

Confirmation

You will receive a confirmation that your filing has been submitted. It will appear in your application tracker on the platform.

2

Officer Review

An OBFC officer reviews your filing and the uploaded financial statements. This review typically takes a few business days.

  • If approved: Your filing proceeds to automatic registration in the databases.
  • If returned: You will be notified with details on what corrections are required. Log in, make the changes, and resubmit.
3

Automatic Registration

Once approved, the system automatically updates both the Companies database and the Accounts database. No further action is required from you.

4

Completion

Your filing moves to the Filings Completed tab, showing the filing date and uploaded statements. The annual report obligation for that year is now fulfilled.

Data Updates

If you submitted an Update of Data request alongside your annual filing, those contact/address changes will be reflected in the Companies Registry once processed.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I file for multiple years at once?

A: Yes. All years with pending filings appear in the Filings Due tab. Upload financial statements for each year and submit them all in one session.

Q: What file format should my financial statements be in?

A: PDF is the recommended format. The system accepts common document formats, but PDF ensures the best compatibility and preserves formatting.

Q: My filing was returned for corrections. What do I do?

A: Log in and check your application tracker. The reviewing officer will have noted what needs correcting. Make the required changes, re-upload documents if needed, and resubmit.

Q: Can I update my company’s address without submitting financial statements?

A: Yes. On the Company Information tab, update your address fields and click “Update contact information”. This submits an Update of Data registration without requiring any financial statement.

Q: I see no entries in “Filings Due”. What does this mean?

A: This means your company is up to date with all annual report obligations. No action is required until the next filing period.

Q: How do I know if my filing has been approved?

A: Track the status through your application tracker on the platform. Once registered in both databases, the filing moves to the Filings Completed tab. You may also receive email notifications about status changes.

Q: What happens if an automated registration step fails?

A: In rare cases, the automatic database registration may encounter an issue. The filing is then routed to a system administrator for resolution. You do not need to take any action — the OBFC team will resolve it.
