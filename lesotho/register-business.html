Register a Business

1. Service Overview

The Register a Business service on the Lesotho One Business Facilitation Centre (OBFC) portal lets you formally register your business with the government entirely online. There are two registration paths depending on the structure you choose:

  • Sole Proprietorship — a business owned and run by one individual. The simplest and fastest route for self-employed traders, hawkers, and small business owners.
  • Company (Pty Ltd or other) — a legally incorporated entity separate from its owners (directors/shareholders). Required for larger operations, foreign investment partnerships, or businesses seeking formal financing.

Both registration types are handled through a single online service at businessregistrations.gov.ls. You select your registration type at the start of the application form.

Who can use this service?

Any individual or group of individuals wishing to formally register a business in the Kingdom of Lesotho. You must have a valid national ID or passport and create a digital account at businessregistrations.gov.ls before applying.

How the System Works

When you log in, the system retrieves your personal details from the national identity database using your national ID number. For company registrations, you enter director information and upload the required documents directly in the form. The system guides you step by step through the process and calculates fees automatically before payment.

2. Before You Start

Make sure you have everything ready before beginning your application. Requirements differ slightly depending on your registration type.

What You Need — Sole Proprietorship

  • A registered account on businessregistrations.gov.ls
  • Your Lesotho National ID number (the system retrieves your personal details automatically)
  • Your chosen business name and the business address in Lesotho
  • A description of your main business activity (e.g. "General Retail Trade", "Tailoring", "Food Vending")
  • A valid payment method (bank card or mobile money) for the LSL 200 registration fee

What You Need — Company

  • A proposed company name (the system checks availability; have 1–2 alternatives ready)
  • National ID or passport of each director (scanned copy, PDF or JPG)
  • Memorandum & Articles of Association (signed, PDF format)
  • The registered office address in Lesotho and intended share capital amount
  • A valid payment method for the total fee of LSL 1,500 (LSL 500 name reservation + LSL 1,000 incorporation)

3. Choose Your Registration Type

At the start of the application form, you will be asked to choose between two registration types. Use the tabs below to understand the key differences before you begin.

Register as a Sole Proprietor

A sole proprietorship is the simplest form of business in Lesotho. It is owned and operated by a single person. There is no legal distinction between the owner and the business — you trade under your own name or a registered trading name.

This option is ideal if you are:

  • A self-employed individual starting a small business (market stall, salon, workshop, etc.)
  • A hawker or general trader in Maseru, Leribe, Berea, or any other district
  • Someone who wants to be legally registered quickly and at low cost

After successful registration, you receive a Business Registration Certificate that you can use to apply for a trading license, open a business bank account, or bid for contracts.

No documents to upload

For sole proprietorship, the system retrieves your personal information directly from the national identity database. You only need to provide your National ID number and fill in your business details.

Incorporate a Company

A company is a separate legal entity from its owners. It can own assets, enter into contracts, and be sued in its own name. The most common type in Lesotho is a Private Company (Pty) Ltd.

This option is ideal if you are:

  • Incorporating with one or more business partners (directors/shareholders)
  • Seeking investment or planning to grow beyond a sole trade
  • Required by a contract, tender, or regulatory body to operate as a registered company
  • A foreign national wishing to do business in Lesotho

After successful registration, you receive a Certificate of Incorporation and a company registration number (CRN) from the OBFC.

Name reservation is required first

Before filling in company details, the system requires you to reserve a company name. This step checks name availability in the OBFC registry. A LSL 500 name reservation fee applies and is non-refundable if the name is approved.

Not sure which to choose?

If you are starting alone with a small operation, choose Sole Proprietorship. If you have partners, need limited liability protection, or have contractual obligations to operate as a company, choose Company. You can always incorporate later as your business grows.

7. After Submission

Once you submit and pay, your application moves through a government review workflow. You can track your status at any time by logging in and opening your application on the portal.

Workflow Timeline

Application Submitted

Your application is received and assigned a reference number. Payment is confirmed.

Immediately after payment

Review Desk

A reviewing officer checks your application for completeness and accuracy. They may send it back to you for corrections, place it on file pending, validate it, or reject it. If sent back, you will receive an email notification with the officer's comments.

Typically 1–2 working days (sole proprietorship)  /  2–4 working days (company)

Approval Desk

A senior approver gives final sign-off on your validated application. The outcome is FILE VALIDATED or FILE REJECT.

Typically 1–2 working days after review

Certificate Generated

After approval, an automated system generates your Business Registration Certificate (sole proprietorship) or Certificate of Incorporation (company) and uploads it to your eRegistrations account for download.

Automatic — happens within minutes of approval

Completion

Your business is officially registered. Download your certificate from the portal dashboard. For companies, your Company Registration Number (CRN) is also available.

Final state

SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS The reviewing officer has found an issue and returned the application Log in, review the officer's comments, make corrections, and resubmit

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to upload any documents for sole proprietorship?

No. The system retrieves your personal information directly from the national identity database when you enter your National ID number. No document uploads are required.

Can I use my personal address as my business address?

Yes, you can use your home address as your registered business address. However, if you operate from a fixed premises (shop, office, workshop), it is recommended to use that address.

Can I register more than one sole proprietorship?

Yes. Each separate business activity must be registered individually. Each registration requires its own application and fee payment.

How do I get my certificate?

After your application is approved, the certificate is automatically generated and uploaded to your account dashboard. Log in, open your application, and click the download link.

What is the minimum number of directors required?

A minimum of one director is required for a Private Company (Pty) Ltd in Lesotho. Public companies may have different requirements.

My proposed company name was rejected. What do I do?

If the name is unavailable or does not meet OBFC naming rules, you will be notified during the reservation step. You can try a different name. The name reservation fee is only charged once a name passes the availability check.

Is the name reservation fee refunded if my incorporation is rejected?

No. The name reservation fee (LSL 500) is non-refundable once the name has been approved and reserved. The incorporation fee (LSL 1,000) refund eligibility depends on the rejection reason — contact the OBFC help desk for your specific case.

Can a foreign national be a director?

Yes. Foreign nationals can be directors of a Lesotho company. You will need to upload a copy of their passport instead of a national ID. At least one director should have a valid address in Lesotho.

What payment methods are accepted?

The portal accepts payment by bank card (Visa, Mastercard) and mobile money. Cash payments are not accepted through the online system.

My payment went through but my application shows as pending. Is this normal?

Yes, it is normal. Payment confirmation may take a few minutes to reflect in the system. If your application is still in "pending payment" status after 30 minutes, contact the OBFC help desk with your payment reference number.

Can I get a refund if my application is rejected?

Refund eligibility depends on the reason for rejection. Contact the OBFC help desk with your application reference number and payment confirmation to discuss your case.

The system does not recognise my National ID number. What should I do?

Ensure you are entering the ID number correctly without spaces or dashes. If the problem persists, your ID may not yet be registered in the national identity database. Contact the OBFC help desk for assistance.

Can I save my progress and come back later?

Yes. The portal auto-saves your in-progress application. You can return to finish it at any time before submitting. Note: for company registration, document uploads are not saved between sessions — you will need to re-upload files if you close the browser.

What browser should I use?

The portal works best on Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari (latest versions). Avoid Internet Explorer.

My document upload keeps failing. What can I do?

Check that your file is in PDF or JPG format and is under the maximum size limit (5 MB for ID documents, 10 MB for the Memorandum & Articles). If the problem continues, try a different browser or contact the help desk.
