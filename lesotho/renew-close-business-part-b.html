1. Service Overview

The Renew or Close a Business service on the Lesotho One Business Facilitation Centre (OBFC) portal allows registered business owners to manage their Business ID entirely online. Applicants can renew their Business ID before it expires or close (terminate) their business registration when they cease operations — covering both:

  • Sole Proprietorships — businesses registered under an individual owner
  • Companies — incorporated entities registered with the OBFC

When the applicant logs in, the system automatically retrieves their Business ID details from the OBFC registry. The applicant selects whether to renew or close their Business ID and completes payment. The back-office processing is then fully automated by two bots running in sequence.

Who can use this service?

Any business owner in Lesotho with an active Business ID registered through the OBFC. The applicant must have a digital account linked to their business at businessregistrations.gov.ls.

How the System Works

Once the applicant submits and pays, no manual officer intervention is required. Two automated bots process the application: the first updates the OBFC business registry database, and the second generates and uploads the renewal certificate or termination confirmation document.

3. Type of Roles

There are two types of roles in an eRegistrations service:

Assigned to a government officer who manually reviews or approves applications. The officer sees the application form, can add comments, and sets a status to move the file forward or send it back.

Role Statuses

Status Meaning
FILE PENDING Application or bot run is queued / in progress. No action required unless it stalls for more than 24 hours.
FILE VALIDATED Desk approved / bot succeeded. Application moves to the next step automatically.
SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS Application returned to applicant with comments. Applicant must correct and resubmit.
FILE REJECT Application permanently rejected. No further processing. Applicant is notified.

Executed automatically by the system — no officer action required. Bots perform tasks such as updating the business registry database or generating certificate PDFs. They run in sequence after the previous step succeeds.

Bot Statuses

Status Meaning Action Required
FILE VALIDATED Bot ran successfully; database updated None — proceeds to BUSINESS ID upload bot
FILE PENDING Bot is queued or running Wait; check again after a few minutes
Bot failed Bot encountered an error during execution See troubleshooting steps below

Troubleshooting: Bot Failed

  1. 1

    Check the bot error log

    Open the application in the back-office and navigate to the BUSINESS ID update bot step. Click the error details to see the specific failure reason.

  2. 2

    Common failure reasons

    • Business ID not found in the OBFC database — The Business ID may have been manually removed. Contact the database administrator.
    • Database connection timeout — Retry the bot; usually resolves on its own.
    • Mapping error — A field mapping may be outdated. Contact the system administrator.
  3. 3

    Retry the bot

    If the error is transient (connection issue), use the Retry action in the bot step. If the error persists after two retries, escalate to the system administrator.

4. Description of Roles

  1. 1

    BUSINESS ID Update

    This bot runs automatically after the citizen submits and pays. It updates the OBFC business registry database with the renewed or terminated Business ID status.

    Depending on the citizen's selection:

    • Renewal: Updates the Business ID's expiry date in the database to the new expiry date calculated during the application. Sets the Business ID status to active/renewed.
    • Termination: Marks the Business ID as closed/cancelled in the database, recording the termination date (today's date at time of processing).
  2. 2

    BUSINESS ID Upload

    Once the BUSINESS ID Update bot completes successfully, this bot generates the updated Business ID certificate PDF and uploads it to the citizen's application record for download.

    • Renewed Business ID: Generates a new Business ID certificate with the updated expiry date, new start date, and renewed status. Attaches it to the application.
    • Terminated Business ID: Generates a termination confirmation document. Attaches it to the application.
    • Uploads all generated documents to the citizen's eRegistrations account so they can be downloaded from the portal.

5. Assistance

Common Issues

Issue Likely Cause Recommended Action
Applicant's Business ID is not shown Account not linked to business, or no Business ID in the database Send back to corrections with instructions to contact OBFC help desk
Incorrect business name shown Database linkage error Reject with note; refer to database administrator
Penalty fee appears incorrect Expiry date or today's date field calculated incorrectly Manually verify using the expiry date and today's date. If the formula is wrong, send back for corrections and flag to system admin.
Payment received but application shows pending payment Payment gateway delay Wait up to 1 hour. If still pending, verify payment with the finance team and manually confirm if appropriate.
