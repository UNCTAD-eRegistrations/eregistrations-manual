Renew or Close a License

Manage your existing trading and industrial licenses online. Renew before they expire or close licenses you no longer need — all in a single application from anywhere in Lesotho.

1. Service Overview

The Renew or Close a License service on the Lesotho One Business Facilitation Centre (OBFC) portal lets you manage your existing business licenses entirely online. You can renew licenses approaching expiry or close licenses you no longer need — covering both:

  • Trading Licenses — for retail, wholesale, hawking, and general trading businesses
  • Industrial Licenses — for manufacturing, processing, and industrial operations

You can process multiple licenses in a single application. For example, if you own a textile factory in Maseru (industrial license) and a hardware shop in Maputsoe (trading license), you can renew one and close the other in the same session and make a single payment.

Who can use this service?

Any registered business owner in Lesotho whose trading or industrial license is active or approaching expiry. You must have an eRegistrations account linked to your business at businessregistrations.gov.ls.

How the System Works

When you log in, the system automatically retrieves your business and license information from the OBFC registry. You do not need to enter your license details manually. The form shows you a summary of your licenses and invites you to select which ones to renew or close.

Depending on the actions you choose, your application is created under the appropriate registration type:

Registration Name License Type Action
Renewal licenses Industrial & Trading Renew
Termination licenses Industrial & Trading Close

The system determines the correct registration automatically based on your selections. You do not need to choose a registration type yourself.

2. Before You Start

What You Need

  • A registered account on businessregistrations.gov.ls linked to your business
  • At least one active trading or industrial license registered to your business
  • A computer or smartphone with internet access
  • A valid payment method (bank card or mobile money) for any applicable fees
Renew early to avoid penalty fees

You can renew a license up to 90 days before its expiry date. If your license has already expired, you may still renew it, but penalty fees will apply for every day past the expiry date. The earlier you renew, the less you pay.

Estimated Processing Time

Action Estimated Time Notes
Renewal 2–3 working days After payment and submission
Closure (Termination) 2–3 working days After payment and submission

3. Renewal vs. Closure

This service supports two distinct actions on your licenses. Use the tabs below to understand the differences before you begin.

Renewing a License

Choose renewal to extend your license for another operating period. When you tick the "Renew" checkbox next to a license, the system will:

  1. Verify the license is eligible for renewal (expiry within 90 days, or already expired)
  2. Calculate the renewal fee based on the license type and sector
  3. Add a penalty fee if the license has already expired (charged per day late)
  4. Display the new start date and new expiry date (typically one year from the renewal date)

After approval and payment, your updated license certificate is generated automatically and uploaded to your eRegistrations account for download.

Closing a License

Choose closure to permanently terminate a license you no longer intend to use. When you tick the "Close" checkbox, the system will:

  1. Record the termination date as today
  2. Calculate any termination processing fee
  3. Add a penalty fee if the license is already expired and has outstanding days
This action cannot be undone

Closing a license is permanent. Once your termination request is approved, the license is cancelled. If you close a bakery license in Teyateyaneng, you will need to apply for a brand-new license to operate that business again.

You can mix and match in one application

You can renew some licenses and close others in the same session. For example, renew your Maseru trading license while closing your Mafeteng industrial license. All fees are combined and paid in a single transaction.

4. Requirements

Account & Information Needed

  • Active account linked to your business entity

Required Documents

No document uploads required

This service does not require you to upload any supporting documents. All license and business information is retrieved automatically from the government database when you log in. Simply select your actions and proceed.

However, it is good practice to keep the following available in case officers request additional information:

  • Your national ID or passport
  • Original license certificate (for reference)
  • Proof of business address (utility bill or lease agreement)
  • Tax clearance certificate, if applicable

5. Fees & Penalties

All fees are calculated automatically by the system based on your license type, business sector, and the number of days past expiry (if any). Fees are displayed in the form before payment. All amounts are in Lesotho Loti (LSL).

Fee Types

Fee Type Description
Renewal fee Standard fee to renew a license for the next period. Amount varies by license category and sector.
Termination fee Administrative fee for permanently closing a license.
Penalty fee 5 LSL per day for each day the license has been expired at the time of renewal or closure. Automatically calculated from the expiry date to today.
Example

’Mme Nthabiseng in Leribe has two trading licenses. One expires in 15 days (no penalty yet) and one expired 30 days ago. She ticks "Renew" for both. The first shows only the standard renewal fee. The second shows the renewal fee plus a penalty for 30 days late. Both fees appear in the Trading Licenses — Renewal Fees panel.

6. Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these steps to complete your license renewal or closure on businessregistrations.gov.ls:

  1. 1

    Log In to Your Account

    Go to businessregistrations.gov.ls and log in with your email address and password. If you do not yet have an account, you must first register as a new user.

    First time?

    If you are logging in for the first time, make sure your account is linked to your business. Contact the OBFC help desk if you cannot see your business or licenses.

  2. 2

    Open the Service

    From your dashboard, find and click the "Renew or Close a License" service card. The system immediately loads your business information and available licenses. You will land on the application form.

  3. 3

    Review Your Business Summary

    The top of the form displays a read-only summary of your registered business pulled from the database. Verify that this is the correct business before proceeding.

    Your Business Summary
    Nthabiseng General Trading
    Sole Proprietor
    General Retail Trade
    31 March 2026

    These fields are read-only — you cannot change them here. If any details are incorrect, contact the OBFC help desk before continuing.

  4. 4

    Choose What You Want to Do

    Select one of the two options using the radio buttons:

    What do you want to do?
    Renew my license(s)
    Extend one or more licenses for another period
    Close (terminate) my license(s)
    Permanently cancel one or more licenses

    You can also choose both within the same application — in that case, select either option first and then use the checkboxes in the license list to mix and match.

  5. 5

    Select Your Licenses

    The List of licenses table shows all licenses registered to your business. For each license, you can check "Renew", "Close", or leave it unchanged.

    List of Licenses
    Name License ID Type Sector Activity Expiry New Expiry Fee Penalty Renew Close
    Nthabiseng Shop MTI-TRD-045 Trading Retail General Retail 31 Jan 2026 31 Jan 2027 LSL 450 LSL 300
    Maseru Factory MTI-IND-012 Industrial Manufacturing Textile Mfg 30 Jun 2026 30 Jun 2027 LSL 850 LSL 0
    Mafeteng Outlet MTI-TRD-078 Trading Wholesale Food Distribution 15 Dec 2025 — LSL 200 LSL 540
    • Tick "Renew" to extend a license. The system fills in the New Expiry, Fee, and Penalty columns automatically.
    • Tick "Close" to permanently terminate a license.
    • Leave both unchecked for licenses you want to leave unchanged.
    • You cannot check both "Renew" and "Close" for the same license at the same time.
  6. 6

    Review the Fees Summary

    After selecting your licenses, scroll down to review the fees summary panels. The system shows separate fee breakdowns for:

    • Industrial licenses being renewed (if any)
    • Trading licenses being renewed (if any)
    • Industrial licenses being terminated (if any)
    • Trading licenses being terminated (if any)

    Each panel lists the per-license fee and penalty, plus a running total. Review carefully before proceeding.

  7. 7

    Proceed to Payment

    Click the "Go to payment" button at the bottom of the form. This takes you to the payment page where you can pay by bank card or mobile money.

    Payment required before submission

    Your application will only be submitted to the government for processing after successful payment. Incomplete payments will leave your application in a pending state.

  8. 8

    Submit Your Application

    After payment, confirm the declaration on the submission page and click "Submit Application". Your application will be sent to the OBFC processing team for review.

    You will receive a confirmation email with your application reference number. Keep this number for tracking.

7. After Submission

Once you submit and pay, your application moves through a government review workflow. You can track your status at any time by logging in and opening your application.

Workflow Timeline

Application Submitted

Your application is received and assigned a reference number. Payment is confirmed.

Immediately after payment

Review Desk

A reviewing officer checks your application for completeness. They may send it back to you for corrections, place it on file pending, validate it, or reject it. If sent back, you will receive an email notification.

Typically 1–3 working days

Approval Desk

A senior approver gives final sign-off on your validated application. The outcome is FILE VALIDATED or FILE REJECT.

Typically 1–2 working days after review

Automated Data Update

After approval, an automated bot (LICENCES update) immediately updates the government database with your new license status — renewing active licenses or marking terminated licenses as closed.

Automatic — happens within minutes

License Documents Generated

Another automated bot (LICENCES upload) generates your updated or renewed license certificates and uploads them to your eRegistrations account.

Automatic — download from your dashboard

Completion

Your renewed license(s) are valid from the new start date. Terminated licenses are officially closed. Download your certificate(s) from the portal.

Final state

Application Status Meanings

Status Meaning Action Required
FILE PENDING Your application is in the queue for review or awaiting additional checks None — wait for processing
SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS The reviewing officer has found an issue and returned the application Log in, review the officer's comments, make corrections, and resubmit
FILE VALIDATED Your application has been approved and processed Download your updated license certificate from the portal
FILE REJECT Your application was rejected Check the reason provided in the portal. Contact OBFC if you need clarification.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

When can I renew my license?

You can renew a license up to 90 days before its expiry date. The system automatically shows the "Renew" option when a license is within this window, or if it has already expired.

My license expired months ago — can I still renew it?

Yes. You can renew an expired license at any time. However, penalty fees will be charged for every day past the expiry date. The penalty amount is calculated and displayed in the form before you pay.

What happens after my renewal is approved?

An automated system updates your license record in the government database immediately and generates a new license certificate. You can download the certificate from your eRegistrations account dashboard.

For how long is a renewed license valid?

Typically one year from the renewal date. The new expiry date is calculated and shown in the license list before you submit.

Can I reverse a closure after submitting?

No. Once your termination application is submitted and approved, the license cancellation is permanent. If you change your mind before the application is approved, contact the OBFC help desk immediately to request withdrawal.

Do I need to pay fees to close a license?

Yes, a small administrative termination fee applies. If the license being closed has already expired, a penalty fee for the overdue period is also charged.

I want to close all my licenses and cease trading entirely. Is that possible?

Yes. You can tick "Close" for all licenses in one application. However, closing your licenses does not automatically cancel your business registration. Contact the OBFC if you wish to deregister your business entirely.

How are penalty fees calculated?

The system counts the number of days from your license expiry date to today and multiplies that by a daily penalty rate. The rate varies by license type and sector. The exact amount is displayed in the form.

Can I pay in cash?

This service requires digital payment through the portal (bank card or mobile money). Cash payments are not accepted through this online system.

My payment went through but my application shows as pending. Is this normal?

Yes, it is normal. Payment confirmation may take a few minutes to reflect. If your application is still in "pending payment" status after 30 minutes, contact the OBFC help desk with your payment confirmation reference.

Can I get a refund if my application is rejected?

Refund eligibility depends on the reason for rejection. Contact the OBFC help desk with your application reference number to discuss your specific situation.

I can log in but I do not see any licenses in the list. What should I do?

This usually means your account is not yet linked to your business, or your licenses are registered under a different account. Contact the OBFC help desk and provide your Business ID or License ID.

The form shows a license that belongs to a different business. What should I do?

Do not proceed. Log out and contact the OBFC help desk immediately. This could indicate a database linkage error that needs to be corrected by an administrator.

Can I save my progress and come back later?

Yes. The portal auto-saves your in-progress applications. You can return to finish your application at any time before submitting. However, fee calculations are based on today's date, so penalty fees may increase if you delay.

What browser should I use?

The portal works best on Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari (latest versions). Avoid Internet Explorer.
