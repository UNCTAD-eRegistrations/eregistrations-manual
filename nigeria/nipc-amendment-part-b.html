NIPC Amendment

Internal processing guide for NIPC officers handling amendment requests to existing company registrations. This manual describes the workflow, role responsibilities, statuses, and processing expectations for the NIPC Amendment service.

1. Service Overview

The NIPC Amendment service allows companies already registered with the Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission to update specific registered details — Company Information, Shareholding & Capital Structure, or Business Activities — without renewing their certificate or filing a new registration.

Who submits this service

Any company holding a valid, active NIPC registration that needs to update one or more registered details. The applicant selects one or more amendment categories at the start of the application — only the fields, documents, and fees for the selected categories apply. Companies without an existing registration must use NIPC Registration instead.

What the client receives

  • An updated NIPC registration record reflecting the amended details only — all other data on file is preserved unchanged.
  • An Amendment Confirmation Letter (PDF) confirming the approved changes, issued digitally and available for download from the client's portal account.
  • Continued, uninterrupted access to the client's existing NIPC certificate and investment incentives.

Information collected from the client

Field / SectionDescription
Amendment categoriesOne or more of: Company Information, Shareholding & Capital, Business Activities. Determines which tabs, documents, and fees apply.
Company InformationUpdated company name, registered office address (State, City, LGA), phone, email, and company contact person.
Shareholding & CapitalAdded, removed, or edited shareholders (Person or Company); updated Local/Foreign Capital (NGN); updated Total of Shares.
Business ActivitiesAdded or removed Sector + ISIC Activity lines; factory/production facility address; current and planned employee counts.
DocumentsCategory-dependent: updated CAC Certificate of Incorporation / Status Report, proof of new address, Letter of Authority, Share Transfer Instrument / Deed of Assignment, Certificate of Capital Importation, updated Memorandum of Association.

Correction loop

The NIPC Amendment Review and NIPC Approval desks can both send the file back to the client for corrections, or reject it entirely. If corrections are requested, the file re-enters the client's queue. Once the client resubmits, the file returns to the desk that requested corrections.

3. Types of Roles

The NIPC Amendment workflow uses two types of roles: human desks (manual review by NIPC officers) and automated bots (system processes that run without human intervention).

Human Desks

Human desk roles require an officer to log in to the portal, review the client's amendment request, and take a decision. Officers can validate the application, send it back for corrections, or reject it. Each decision triggers a notification to the client.

StatusMeaning
FILE PENDING The file has arrived at this desk and is awaiting review by an officer.
FILE VALIDATED The officer has reviewed and approved the file. It proceeds to the next step in the workflow.
SEND BACK TO CORRECTIONS The officer has identified issues and returned the file to the client with a request for corrections. The client must make changes and resubmit.
FILE REJECT The officer has permanently rejected the amendment request. The client is notified. A new amendment application must be started to restart the process.

Automated Bots

Bot roles execute automatically when triggered by the workflow — no officer action is needed. Bots handle treasury record creation, registry updates, and confirmation letter generation. If a bot fails, the issue must be investigated and the bot re-run.

StatusMeaningAction Required
FILE PENDING The bot has been triggered and is processing. None — wait for completion.
FILE VALIDATED The bot completed its task successfully. The file moves to the next step. None.
BOT FAILED The bot encountered an error and could not complete its task. Investigate the error in the system logs. Fix the underlying issue and re-run the bot, or contact technical support.

4. Description of Roles

The NIPC Amendment workflow has 5 processing roles after client submission. Below is a description of what each role does and what to check.

  1. 1

    NIPC Treasury Automated Bot

    This bot runs immediately after the client submits the amendment and completes payment. It creates a treasury record in the NIPC financial system for the amendment fee paid (the sum of the fees for each category the client selected: ₦10,000 Company Information, ₦20,000 Shareholding & Capital, ₦10,000 Business Activities).

    If it fails: Check that the client's payment was received and the payment reference is valid. Contact the technical team to investigate the treasury integration.

  2. 2

    NIPC Amendment Review Human Desk

    An NIPC officer reviews the client's amendment request. This is the primary quality control step — only the categories the client selected should have changed; all other data on the existing record must remain untouched.

    What to check:

    • Only the fields within the client's selected categories differ from the existing record.
    • Company Information (if selected): the updated CAC Certificate of Incorporation or CAC Status Report reflects the new name or address; a Letter of Authority or Power of Attorney is present if a representative other than a director submitted the request.
    • Shareholding & Capital (if selected): the Share Transfer Instrument / Deed of Assignment matches the declared shareholding changes; the updated CAC Status Report shows the new shareholding; shareholder shares do not exceed the declared Total of Shares; the Certificate of Capital Importation matches any declared increase in foreign capital.
    • Business Activities (if selected): the updated Memorandum of Association matches the new activities, if the change was reflected in the MEMART; the declared Sector and ISIC Activity codes are valid.

    Available actions: Validate (→ NIPC Approval), Request Corrections (→ Client), Reject.

  3. 3

    NIPC Approval Human Desk

    A senior NIPC officer performs the final review and gives official approval to the amendment.

    What to do:

    • Review the application and the notes left by the NIPC Amendment Review desk.
    • Confirm all category-specific documents are in order and consistent with the requested changes.
    • Confirm the amendment does not conflict with another pending Amendment or an in-progress Renewal on the same registration.

    Available actions: Validate (→ NIPC Registry), Request Corrections (→ Client), Reject.

  4. 4

    NIPC Registry Automated Bot

    This bot runs immediately after NIPC Approval. It updates only the amended fields in the official NIPC registry database — company data outside the client's selected categories is left unchanged.

    If it fails: Check connectivity to the NIPC registry system. Contact the technical team if the issue persists.

  5. 5

    Amendment Confirmation Automated Bot

    This bot generates the client's Amendment Confirmation Letter as a PDF listing the approved changes, and uploads it to the client's portal account. The client is automatically notified when the document is available for download.

    If it fails: Check that all required data was correctly saved during the Approval step. Re-run the bot once the data issue is resolved.

5. Assistance

Common processing issues

IssueLikely CauseResolution
NIPC Treasury bot failed Payment not fully processed, invalid payment reference, or treasury API unavailable. Verify payment receipt in the financial system. Contact the technical team to re-run the bot.
Changed data outside the selected categories Client edited fields that were not part of the categories they selected. Send back for corrections and request the client to restrict changes to the selected categories, or select the additional category that applies.
Share Transfer Instrument doesn't match declared shareholding The uploaded document doesn't reconcile with the shareholder table entered in the form. Send back for corrections requesting a consistent document and figures.
Shareholder shares exceed total shares Client listed more shares for shareholders than the declared Total of Shares. Send back for corrections and request the client to reconcile the shareholder table with the total shares figure.
CAC documents don't reflect the claimed name/address change Client has not yet completed the corresponding filing with CAC. Reject or send back for corrections, directing the client to complete the CAC filing first, then reapply.
CCI amount doesn't match declared foreign capital increase Client declared a different foreign capital figure than what's on the CCI. Send back for corrections with a request to reconcile the figures and provide an updated CCI if needed.
Registration not found or not active The NIPC registration number entered is invalid, or the underlying certificate has lapsed. Reject and direct the client to NIPC Registration (if never registered) or NIPC Renewal (if the certificate has expired).
Amendment Confirmation bot failed Missing or inconsistent data from the Approval step. Return to the NIPC Approval desk to verify all fields are populated correctly, then re-run the bot.
