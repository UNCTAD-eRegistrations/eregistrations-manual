NIPC Renewal

Renew your NIPC pioneer status certificate online. Keep your investment registration active and continue to enjoy NIPC incentives and benefits in Nigeria. This guide walks you through every step.

1. Overview

The NIPC Renewal service allows companies already registered with the Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission to renew their NIPC pioneer status certificate before it expires. Keeping your certificate current ensures you continue to benefit from investment incentives, tax reliefs, and other privileges provided under Nigerian investment law.

Who should use this service

Any company that holds a valid NIPC registration certificate and needs to renew it. Your company's core details (name, RC number, NIPC registration number) are already on file — you only need to update your current investment figures and employee count.

Renew before expiry

Apply for renewal before your current certificate expires. Late applications may require additional review. Check your expiry date in the portal before starting.

What you will receive

  • A renewed NIPC Certificate issued digitally and downloadable from your portal account.
  • An updated NIPC registration record reflecting your latest investment and employment data.
  • Continued access to all NIPC investment incentives and facilitation services.

2. Before You Start

Prepare the following before you begin. Having everything ready allows you to complete the form in one session.

  • NIPC portal login credentials — your username and password for the NIPC eRegistrations portal.
  • Current number of employees — total headcount at the time of renewal.
  • Updated investment figures — domestic and foreign investment amounts in both NGN and USD.
  • Country of origin for the primary investment.
  • Start date of operation — the date your company began operating under NIPC registration.
  • PenCom certificate (if your company is registered with PenCom) — a PDF or image scan of your current pension compliance certificate.
  • Certificate of Capital Importation (CCI) (if available) — issued by your Nigerian bank to confirm foreign capital inflows.
Pre-filled fields

Your company name, RC number, NIPC registration number, email, and original issue date are already pre-filled from your existing record. You do not need to re-enter these.

4. Payment & Submit

After completing the Update and Documents tabs, proceed to Payment and then Send to formally submit your application.

  1. 1

    Review your application

    Before paying, review all information you have entered — investment figures, employee count, and uploaded documents. Use the Back button to correct anything before proceeding.

  2. 2

    Proceed to payment

    Click Go to Payment. The system will calculate the renewal fee based on your investment data. You will see the amount due before confirming.

  3. 3

    Complete payment

    Pay the renewal fee online using your debit card, bank transfer, or other accepted methods. Keep your payment confirmation reference number for your records.

  4. 4

    Submit your application

    After payment, click Send to formally submit your renewal application. You will receive a confirmation with your application reference number.

Payment processed automatically

After payment is submitted, an automated bot will create a treasury record within minutes. No action is required — just wait for the NIPC review team to process your application.

5. After Submission

Once submitted, your renewal application enters the NIPC processing workflow. Here is what happens at each stage:

NIPC Treasury Automated

An automated system creates a treasury record for your payment immediately after submission. No action is required from you.

Typical duration: 5–15 minutes

NIPC Review Human Review

An NIPC officer reviews your application — checking investment figures, employee count, and uploaded documents for accuracy and compliance. If corrections are needed, you will receive a notification to make changes and resubmit.

Typical duration: 3–7 business days

NIPC Approval Senior Approval

A senior NIPC officer reviews and approves the renewed registration. The new renewal date and expiry date for your certificate are set at this stage.

Typical duration: 2–4 business days

NIPC Registry Automated

An automated system updates the official NIPC registry with your renewed registration data. This happens immediately after approval.

Typical duration: A few minutes

Certificate Generation Automated

Your renewed NIPC Certificate is automatically generated as a PDF and uploaded to your portal account. You will receive a notification when it is ready.

Typical duration: A few minutes

Certificate Available

Log in to the NIPC portal, open your renewal application, and download your new certificate. It will reflect your updated investment data and new expiry date.

If corrections are requested

If the NIPC reviewer sends your application back for corrections, you will receive an email notification. Log in to the portal, review the reviewer's comments, make the requested changes, and resubmit. Do not start a new application — always continue from your existing one.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

How often do I need to renew my NIPC registration?
NIPC pioneer status certificates have a fixed validity period. You must renew before the expiry date shown on your current certificate. Log in to your portal account to check your current expiry date at any time.
My company has no foreign investment. Do I still need to answer the CCI question?
Yes, the question is required. Simply select No — "CCI has NOT been made available." You will not be asked to upload a CCI document and your application will proceed normally.
What is PenCom and does my company need to register?
PenCom (the National Pension Commission) regulates pension fund administration in Nigeria. Companies with 3 or more employees are required to enrol them in a Contributory Pension Scheme (CPS). If your company has 3+ employees, you should be registered with PenCom. Visit pencom.gov.ng for more information on registration.
My investment figures have changed significantly. Is that a problem?
No — the renewal process is designed to capture updated figures. Enter your current accurate investment amounts. The NIPC reviewer will compare them with your previous record. Significant changes may prompt clarification questions from the reviewer, but will not automatically delay your renewal.
How long does the entire renewal process take?
The total processing time is typically 5–15 business days from the date of submission, assuming all information is correct and no corrections are needed. NIPC Review takes 3–7 days, Approval takes 2–4 days, and certificate generation after approval is automated (minutes).
I submitted my application but made a mistake. Can I correct it?
Once submitted, you cannot edit your application directly. The NIPC reviewer will send it back for corrections if an issue is found. If the error is critical and the application hasn't been reviewed yet, contact NIPC support immediately using the details in Section 9.
Where can I download my renewed certificate?
Once approved and generated, you will receive an email notification. Log in to the NIPC portal, open your renewal application, and look for the Documents section — your renewed NIPC Certificate will be available as a downloadable PDF.
