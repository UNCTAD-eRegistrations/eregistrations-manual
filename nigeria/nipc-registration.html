User Manual

NIPC Registration

Register your company with the Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission (NIPC) to access investment incentives and operate legally in Nigeria. This guide walks you through every step of the online application.

Start Application →

Table of Contents

1 Overview 2 Before You Start 3 Required Documents 4 Step-by-Step Process 5 After Submission

1. Overview

The NIPC Registration service allows companies — both domestic and foreign — to register with the Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission. Registration with NIPC grants access to investment incentives, pioneer status benefits, and other investment facilitation services provided by the Nigerian government.

Who should register

Any company incorporated under Nigerian law (with a CAC Certificate of Incorporation) that wishes to benefit from NIPC investment incentives, or any foreign investor operating in Nigeria, should complete this registration.

The entire process is completed online through the NIPC eRegistrations portal. Once your application is reviewed and approved, you will receive your NIPC Registration Certificate digitally.

2. Before You Start

Make sure you have the following ready before beginning your application:

  • A valid email address — you will use it to create or log in to your account.
  • Your company's RC number (Corporate Affairs Commission registration number).
  • Details about your company's capital structure (local and foreign capital amounts, total shares).
  • Information on shareholders (name, nationality, number of shares for each significant shareholder).
  • Details about your investment activities (sector, ISIC activity codes, employee headcount, investment amounts in NGN and USD).
  • All required documents in digital format — see Section 3 below.
Important

Your company must not already be registered with NIPC. If your company has an existing NIPC registration, use the NIPC Renewal or NIPC Amendment services instead.

Accepted file formats

Documents may be uploaded as: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP, DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX. Maximum file size per document: 5 MB.

3. Required Documents

The following documents must be uploaded during the application. Prepare scanned copies or digital versions in advance.

  • Power of Attorney or Letter of Authority — authorising the person submitting the application to act on behalf of the company.
  • CAC Certificate of Incorporation — issued by the Corporate Affairs Commission.
  • CAC Status Report — current status report from the Corporate Affairs Commission.
  • Memorandum of Association — the company's memorandum and articles of association.
  • PenCom Certificate — Pension Commission (PenCom) compliance certificate (required if the company has 5 or more employees).
  • Certificate of Capital Importation (CCI) — issued by a Nigerian bank, required when foreign investment has been brought into the country.
PenCom certificate

If your company currently has fewer than 5 employees, the PenCom Certificate field will not be required. If you have 5 or more employees and are not yet registered with PenCom, you should complete that registration before applying to NIPC.

4. Step-by-Step Process

The NIPC Registration application is divided into the following stages:

  1. 1

    Create an Account / Log In

    Go to the NIPC eRegistrations portal. If you don't have an account yet, click Register and complete the sign-up process. If you already have an account, click Login.

  2. 2

    Start the NIPC Registration Application

    From your dashboard, select the NIPC Registration service and click Start new application. The application form will open with a Guide tab explaining what is needed.

  3. 3

    Complete the Form

    Fill in the four tabs in order. Use Next and Previous to navigate between them.

    Applicant Information

    Information about the person submitting on behalf of the company.

    FieldRequiredDescription
    Relationship with the company Yes Select whether you are an Executive (director, CEO, etc.) or an Employee acting as representative.
    Position Yes Your job title (shown if Executive).
    Occupation Yes Your occupation (shown if Employee).
    Phone Yes Your contact phone number.

    Company Information

    Enter details as they appear on the CAC Certificate of Incorporation.

    Identity & Address
    FieldRequiredDescription
    RC Number Yes CAC registration number. The system verifies it against the NIPC registry.
    Company Name Yes Full legal name as registered with CAC.
    Address, State, City, Local Government Area Yes Registered office address in Nigeria.
    Phone & Email Yes Official company contact details.
    Capital Structure
    FieldRequiredDescription
    Local Capital Yes Capital contributed by Nigerian investors (NGN).
    Foreign Capital Yes Capital contributed by foreign investors (NGN).
    Total Amount Auto Calculated automatically.
    Total of Shares Yes Total number of issued shares.
    % Local / % Foreign Capital Auto Calculated automatically.
    Shareholders

    Add each significant shareholder via the List of shareholders table. For each row, specify whether the shareholder is a Person (first name, last name, nationality, shares) or a Company (name, country of registration, registration number, shares).

    Share total validation

    The sum of all shareholders' shares must not exceed the Total of Shares. The form will warn you if exceeded.

    Company Contact Person

    Provide the name, function, phone and email of the main company contact (e.g. CEO or Company Secretary).

    Investment Details

    Factory / Production Facility
    FieldRequiredDescription

    Same address as the main office? Yes If No, fill in the factory's address separately.
    Business Activities

    Add one or more activity lines. For each, select the Sector and the corresponding ISIC Activity.

    Employees

    Are they already registered with PenCom? Conditional Shown if 5 or more employees. If Yes, PenCom certificate required in Documents tab.

    Investment Amounts
    FieldRequiredDescription
    Domestic Investment (NGN & USD) Yes Investment funded from within Nigeria.
    Foreign Investment (NGN & USD) Yes Investment brought from abroad.
    Total Investment (NGN & USD) Auto Calculated automatically.

    Certificate of Capital Importation (CCI)
    FieldRequiredDescription

    Document Upload

    Upload the required supporting documents. Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP, DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX (max 5 MB each).

    DocumentRequiredNotes
    Power of Attorney Yes Authorises the applicant to act on behalf of the company. A Letter of Authority is also acceptable.
    CAC Certificate of Incorporation Yes Original certificate issued by the Corporate Affairs Commission.
    CAC Status Report Yes Current status report from CAC.
    Memorandum of Association Yes Company's Memorandum and Articles of Association.
    PenCom Certificate Yes* Required if the company has 5 or more employees registered with PenCom.
    Certificate of Capital Importation Yes* Required if the CCI has been made available (as answered in the Investment tab).

    * Conditional — required depending on answers in the Investment tab.

    Once all documents are uploaded, click Go to payment.

  4. 4

    Pay the Registration Fee

    After completing the form you are directed to the Payment page. Select your preferred payment method and complete the payment.

    Fee ItemAmount (NGN)Notes
    NIPC Business Registration 150,000 Non-refundable
    Total to pay 150,000
    Non-refundable fee

    Ensure all information is accurate before paying. The fee is non-refundable. After successful payment a receipt number is generated and your application enters the review queue.

  5. 5

    Sign the Declaration and Submit

    On the final Send page, read the solemn declaration carefully, check the box to confirm the information is true and correct, then click Submit application.

  6. 6

    Track Your Application

    Follow your application's progress from My Dashboard. You will be notified if corrections are required or when your application has been approved.

  7. 7

    Download Your Certificate

    Upon approval, your NIPC Registration Certificate and Registration Receipt are available for download from your dashboard.

5. After Submission

Once your application is submitted and payment confirmed, it enters the NIPC review workflow. Below is an overview of what happens at each stage.

Application Submitted & Payment Confirmed

Your application is received. A reference number is assigned and your file enters the review queue.

Day 0

NIPC Initial Review

An NIPC officer reviews your application for completeness and accuracy. They may validate it, send it back for corrections, or reject it with reasons.

Processing

CAC Verification

A CAC officer verifies your company details (RC number, Certificate of Incorporation, etc.) against the CAC registry.

Processing

NIPC Approval

A senior NIPC officer gives final approval to your registration.

Processing

Registration & Certificate Generation

Your company is recorded in the NIPC registry and your NIPC Registration Certificate and Registration Receipt are generated automatically.

Automated

Certificate Available for Download

You receive a notification and can download your certificate from My Dashboard.

Final step
Corrections requested

If an officer sends your application back for corrections, you will receive a notification. Log in to your dashboard, make the required changes, and resubmit. Your application will then re-enter the review process.
